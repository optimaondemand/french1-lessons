⚜ Être français — What It Means to Be French
Lesson · about 50 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
What does it mean to be French — and how has your own understanding grown this year?
This is the last module of French 1, and it gathers the whole year. You will describe, compare, narrate the past, and reflect. Today you meet the ten words that name a country's identity — its language, its culture, its motto: Liberté, Égalité, Fraternité (Liberty, Equality, Fraternity). Hear each one spoken before you say it back. To finish, you'll open the Carte de France one last time and lock in the regions.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce ten words about French identity and the Republic.
2
I will be able to match each spoken word to its meaning by ear.
3
I will be able to locate the 13 metropolitan regions of France on the map.

⏰ PART 1 · SE METTRE EN ROUTE 12 min

A year, in one question

Over two semesters you introduced yourself, described your family, told time, talked about food and leisure, visited Paris and the regions. Now you meet the country as an idea: a language, a culture, a set of shared values. Before the vocabulary, a moment of honesty about your own journey.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Looking back over the whole year, what has learning French given you most?
📚 PART 2 · L'IDENTITÉ ET LA RÉPUBLIQUE 18 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 on each word and say it back out loud. These ten name what a nation shares — its language, its culture, and the three words on every town hall in France: Liberté, Égalité, Fraternité. Just get them in your ear today.

  • être françaisto be French
  • l'identitéidentity
  • la cultureculture
  • la languelanguage
  • la traditiontradition
  • la libertéliberty
  • l'égalitéequality
  • la fraternitéfraternity / brotherhood
  • la devisethe motto
  • le drapeauthe flag
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Notice the family of words ending in -té: identité, liberté, égalité, fraternité. In English these become -ty (identity, liberty). Say each French word three times, keeping the stress light and even — French doesn't hammer the last syllable the way English does.
📝 PART 3 · RÉVISE TON VOCABULAIRE 10 min

Drill the words with the Vocabulaire page

Open the Module 9 Vocabulaire study page (in this module) — every word, its meaning, and its audio in one place, as flip cards you can drill by ear. Because your Vocabulary Check is listening-based, practice by hearing the word and recalling its meaning.

Making your own paper or digital cards on top of that is a great memory booster — but it’s optional, not required.
