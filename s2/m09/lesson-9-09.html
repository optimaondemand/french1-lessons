Lesson 9.09
⚜ Carte de France & VR Soirée — Explore & Celebrate
Lesson · about 40 minutes · ungraded practice
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A breath before the final quiz — master the map of France, then celebrate the year at the VR Soirée.
Both of today's activities are ungraded. The map is your last chance to lock in the geography before the Carte de France mastery extra-credit challenge (in this module's assessments). The VR Soirée is your semester project — a French evening where the whole class shows what a year of French can do.
🗺️ La Carte de France
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
EXPLORER + QUIZ MODES 15 min
  • Explorer mode: hover and click to review the 13 metropolitan regions — Île-de-France, Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, and the rest.
  • Quiz mode: timed click-to-find. Your score, errors, and time are shown — restart as often as you like, and try to name la plus grande region and la plus petite.
  • This is the geography you've built all year. Mastering all 13 feeds the once-per-semester Carte extra-credit challenge — worth up to +1% on your semester grade — which is part of this module's assessments.
▶ Open the Carte de France
🥽 La Soirée française · VR semester project
SEMESTER PROJECT · VR
ENGAGE · CLASS CELEBRATION 25 min
  • The VR Soirée is the semester project: an evening gathering in ENGAGE where the class comes together to celebrate a year of French.
  • Come ready to speak: greet others, introduce yourself, share one thing you discovered this year (j'ai découvert…), and one thing you would like to do next (je voudrais…).
  • Use the room as your stage: the space is dressed with French symbols — the drapeau tricolore, images of Marianne, the motto. Point them out and name them.
  • This is a joyful, low-stakes performance of everything you can now do. Speak freely; being understood matters more than being perfect.

Open ENGAGE from your headset or desktop app and join the Soirée française Location as directed in Canvas. Full project instructions and any submission are posted with the assignment.

📝 PART 3 · NOTE RAPIDE 2 min

Tomorrow is the Grammar Quiz — the last graded piece of Module 9, and it includes past-tense listening items. Tonight, review the superlative and listen to the synthesis clip and one passage. That's all the prep you need.

⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +8 min
Post-Soirée reflection: write 2–3 sentences about what felt natural when you spoke French with others, and one thing you're proud of. Naming it makes it real — and it's great material for the Final's reflection.
