🎤 PART 1 · PHONÉTIQUE 13 min

The “-té” ending — short, clean, unstressed

French words ending in -té (English -ty) end on a light, closed vowel — like “ay” cut short. Don't drag it, and don't add an English “-tee.” Keep the stress even across the whole word.
  • l'identitéidentity
  • la libertéliberty
  • l'égalitéequality
  • la fraternitéfraternity

The superlative « plus »

Inside the superlative, plus before an adjective is usually said “plu” — the final s stays silent. Listen for how la plus grande glides. In le meilleur, land clearly on the « -eur » ending.
  • la plus grande villeplus + adjective
  • le moins chermoins + adjective
  • le meilleur restaurantthe best (irregular)

Shadow it: play each clip, pause, and copy the exact rhythm three times. Aim to match the light French stress, not the English one.

⚡ Défi éclair
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
BUILD IT YOURSELF 5 min
Build each one in your head — or out loud — before you reveal the answer.
⚡ DÉFI 1
Say it in French: It is the most beautiful museum in the city.
C’est le plus beau musée de la ville.
The superlative keeps its article, and de means “in”.
⚡ DÉFI 2
Say it in French: One day I would like to go back to France.
Un jour, je voudrais retourner en France.
voudrais — polite, not je veux.
⚡ DÉFI 3
Say it in French: She is proud to speak French.
Elle est fière de parler français.
fier → fière, then de + infinitive.
