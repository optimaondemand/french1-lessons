Lesson 9.03
⚜ Je voudrais… — Polite Wishes & Requests
Lesson · about 50 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
One verb form turns a blunt demand into a courteous request — and it's the most useful word a traveler owns.
You met je voudrais in the food module. Today you formalize it. Voudrais is the soft, polite way to say what you want — a wish (je voudrais visiter la France) or a request (je voudrais un café, s'il vous plaît). Courtesy is one of Optima's seven virtues; in French, it lives in this verb. Then you'll practise voudrais alongside yesterday's superlative, and build three superlatives from memory.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to use voudrais to make a polite request (+ a noun) and a wish (+ an infinitive).
2
I will be able to recognize all six forms of vouloir (to want) in this polite (conditional) pattern.
3
I will be able to build superlatives from memory and choose the polite voudrais in short practice items.
🎵 PART 1 · POURQUOI « VOUDRAIS » ? 10 min

The polite cousin of “je veux”

The idea. Je veux (“I want”) can sound blunt — even rude — to a stranger. Je voudrais (“I would like”) is soft and polite. Use it whenever you order, ask, or express a hope. Two patterns: voudrais + a noun (a request) or voudrais + an infinitive (a wish).

Listen to the two patterns side by side:

  • Je voudrais un café, s'il vous plaît.request: voudrais + noun
  • Je voudrais visiter toute la France un jour.I would like to visit all of France one day — wish: voudrais + infinitive · toute la France = all of France
📋 PART 2 · LE TABLEAU COMPLET — VOULOIR (POLI) 12 min

All six persons of the polite form

This is the conditional of vouloir. You'll use je voudrais and tu voudrais most; read the whole table aloud so all six are familiar.

PersonFormMeaning
jeje voudraisI would like
tutu voudraisyou would like
il / elle / onil voudraithe / she / one would like
nousnous voudrionswe would like
vousvous voudriezyou (formal/plural) would like
ils / ellesils voudraientthey would like
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +6 min
Pair voudrais with the words you already own: order at a café (je voudrais un croissant), plan a trip (je voudrais visiter le Louvre), or make a wish for next year (je voudrais mieux parler français). Write one of each.
✍️ PART 3 · À TON TOUR 8 min

Turn the blunt line into a polite one, and write one wish of your own. Commit to an answer first, then reveal to check.

🧠 MAKE IT POLITE
1) Rewrite “Je veux un thé.” as a polite request.   2) Write a wish with voudrais + infinitive about the future.
1) RequestJe voudrais un thé, s'il vous plaît.
2) WishJe voudrais retourner en France un jour.
Your wish can name any infinitive you know — visiter, parler, découvrir, voir. If you added s'il vous plaît to the request and used an infinitive after voudrais, you've got it.

Seven items on Module 9’s grammar: building superlatives, and using je voudrais politely.
✎ ITEM 1

✎ ITEM 2

✎ ITEM 3

✎ ITEM 4

✎ ITEM 5

✎ ITEM 6
Which is the polite way to order?
✎ ITEM 7
Paris et Lyon sont ______ grandes villes de France. (pl.)
📋 PART 4 · LE SUPERLATIF, ENCORE UNE FOIS 13 min

From memory, complete these three superlatives. Commit first — then reveal.

🧠 COMPLÈTE · le superlatif
1) “the largest city in France”   2) “the best restaurant”   3) “the least expensive museum”
1)la plus grande ville de France
2)le meilleur restaurant
3)le musée le moins cher
Remember: “the best” is le meilleur, never “le plus bon.” If all three matched, the superlative is yours.
