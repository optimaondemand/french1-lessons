Lesson 9.03
⚜ Je voudrais… — Polite Wishes & Requests
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
One verb form turns a blunt demand into a courteous request — and it's the most useful word a traveler owns.
You met je voudrais in the food module. Today you formalize it. Voudrais is the soft, polite way to say what you want — a wish (je voudrais visiter la France) or a request (je voudrais un café, s'il vous plaît). Courtesy is one of Optima's seven virtues; in French, it lives in this verb.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to use voudrais to make a polite request (+ a noun) and a wish (+ an infinitive).
2
I will be able to recognize all six forms of vouloir in this polite (conditional) pattern.
🎵 PART 1 · POURQUOI « VOUDRAIS » ? 10 min

The polite cousin of “je veux”

The idea. Je veux (“I want”) can sound blunt — even rude — to a stranger. Je voudrais (“I would like”) is soft and polite. Use it whenever you order, ask, or express a hope. Two patterns: voudrais + a noun (a request) or voudrais + an infinitive (a wish).

Listen to the two patterns side by side:

  • Je voudrais un café, s'il vous plaît.request: voudrais + noun
  • Je voudrais visiter toute la France un jour.wish: voudrais + infinitive
📋 PART 2 · LE TABLEAU COMPLET — VOULOIR (POLI) 12 min

All six persons of the polite form

This is the conditional of vouloir. You'll use je voudrais and tu voudrais most; read the whole table aloud so all six are familiar.

PersonFormMeaning
jeje voudraisI would like
tutu voudraisyou would like
il / elle / onil voudraithe / she / one would like
nousnous voudrionswe would like
vousvous voudriezyou (formal/plural) would like
ils / ellesils voudraientthey would like
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +6 min
Pair voudrais with the words you already own: order at a café (je voudrais un croissant), plan a trip (je voudrais visiter le Louvre), or make a wish for next year (je voudrais parler mieux français). Write one of each.
✍️ PART 3 · À TON TOUR 8 min

Turn the blunt line into a polite one, and write one wish of your own. Commit to an answer first, then reveal to check.

🧠 MAKE IT POLITE
1) Rewrite “Je veux un thé.” as a polite request.   2) Write a wish with voudrais + infinitive about the future.
1) RequestJe voudrais un thé, s'il vous plaît.
2) WishJe voudrais retourner en France un jour.
Your wish can name any infinitive you know — visiter, parler, découvrir, voir. If you added s'il vous plaît to the request and used an infinitive after voudrais, you've got it.
