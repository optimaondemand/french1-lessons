⚜ La fierté & le superlatif — Saying “the most” and “the best”
Lesson · about 50 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you named the country. Today you learn to rank it — the largest city, the most beautiful place, the best restaurant.
First, ten more words about pride, country, and citizenship. Then the superlative: how French says the most (le plus), the least (le moins), and the one irregular you must know — le meilleur, the best. In Module 8 you compared with plus…que; the superlative is the top of that ladder. You'll close with your first try at typing the module's words, accents and articles included.

🧠 PART 2 · LA GRAMMAIRE — LE SUPERLATIF 17 min

The most, the least, the best

The rule. Put le / la / les (matching the noun) + plus (most) or moins (least) + the adjective. Add de… for “in / of”: la plus grande ville de France = the largest city in France. de + le → du (of the): le meilleur restaurant du quartier = the best restaurant in the neighborhood.
IdeaFormationExample
the most…le/la/les plus + adj.la plus grande ville
the least…le/la/les moins + adj.le moins cher
the best (irregular)le meilleur / la meilleurele meilleur restaurant
“in / of”… de + place… de France

One trap: bon (good) does not become “le plus bon.” The best is always le meilleur. Hear all three:

  • Paris est la plus grande ville de France.Paris is the largest city in France.
  • C'est le musée le moins cher de la ville.It's the least expensive museum in the city.
  • C'est le meilleur restaurant du quartier.It's the best restaurant in the neighborhood.
