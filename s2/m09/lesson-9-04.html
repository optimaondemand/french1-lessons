Lesson 9.04
⚜ Synthèse — Narrate Your Whole Year
Lesson · about 50 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Everything you learned this year, in one paragraph: what you did, what things were like, and how far you've come.
This is the synthesis. You'll weave together the passé composé (completed actions: j'ai commencé) and the imparfait (how things were: j'étais nerveux = I was nervous), drop in an object pronoun (je la comprends), and rank with a comparative (mieux qu'avant). Then you'll write your own year, listen to a dictation and write down what you hear, and finish by typing superlative, voudrais, and past-tense forms the way the quizzes ask for them.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to combine passé composé, imparfait, an object pronoun, and a comparative in one short narration.
2
I will be able to understand a spoken year-in-review sentence well enough to write it down.
3
I will be able to type superlative, voudrais, and past-tense forms correctly, accents included.
✅ PART 1 · CHOISIS LA BONNE FORME 12 min

Four choices, drawing on the whole year. Instant feedback — practice, not a grade.

✎ ITEM 1 · passé composé vs. imparfait
A completed action last year: “L'année dernière, j'______ le français.” (commencer)
✎ ITEM 2 · imparfait for “how it was”
Describing how you felt back then: “Au début (at the start), j'______ nerveux.” (être)
✎ ITEM 3 · object pronoun
“J'aime la culture française et je ______ comprends mieux maintenant.”
✎ ITEM 4 · comparative
“Maintenant, je parle ______ qu'au début de l'année.” (better)
✍️ PART 2 · À TOI D'ÉCRIRE 13 min

Write four sentences about your year in French

In your notebook, narrate your year. Use at least one passé composé (something you did), one imparfait (how it was at the start), one object pronoun, and one comparative (mieux, plus, le plus).

Model: L'année dernière, j'ai commencé le français et j'étais nerveux. J'ai visité Paris et j'ai découvert la cuisine. J'aime la culture française et je la comprends mieux maintenant. Un jour, je voudrais voir les plus beaux endroits du pays.

Keep this — you'll reuse it for your Speaking Task on Day 7 and for the Semester 2 Final. Check each sentence against the four elements above.

🎧 PART 3 · LA DICTÉE 15 min

Hear it, type it, check it

Play each clip (as many times as you need). Type exactly what you hear — accents and all — then press Check. Use Reveal only after you have tried. Don't reveal early; the struggle is the learning.

Accents: — click a box first, then a letter.
1A superlative — listen for « la plus ».
Paris est la plus grande ville de France.
2The irregular “best”.
C'est le meilleur restaurant du quartier.
3A polite wish with voudrais.
Je voudrais visiter toute la France un jour.
4Past narration — catch the passé composé and the imparfait.
L'année dernière, j'ai commencé le français et j'étais nerveuse.
5Object pronoun + comparative together.
J'aime la culture française et je la comprends mieux maintenant.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +10 min
Spiral all the way back: pick one thing from each earlier module (a routine, a meal, a leisure activity, a place in Paris) and narrate it in the past — l'année dernière, j'ai… et c'était… Mixing old topics with new tenses is exactly what the Final Exam asks of you.
✏️ Écris la forme — les superlatifs, voudrais et le passé
