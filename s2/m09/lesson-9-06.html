1 · Vocabulary Check
25 pts · 1 attempt
COMPLETED IN CANVAS · AUTO-GRADED · should be submitted by the end of the module
  • Listening-based. About three-quarters of the items are ear-only: hear a word, choose or type the meaning; some are dictation (write what you hear).
  • One attempt, no retakes — so warm up your ear first (see the check-in above).
  • Scope: all 20 Module 9 words (identity, the Republic, pride, and the superlative markers). Headphones recommended; you can replay each clip.
  • Auto-graded — you'll see your score immediately.

2 · Langue en action
20 pts
COMPLETED IN CANVAS · AUTO-GRADED · should be submitted by the end of the module
  • Structured production. Three parts: form superlatives in context, use voudrais for polite wishes, and short comprehension from Être français.
  • Auto-graded (fill-in-the-blank, multiple choice, passage items).
  • Lean on your Day 4 and Day 5 sentences — you've already written most of what this asks.
