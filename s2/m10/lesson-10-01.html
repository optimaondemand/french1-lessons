L’examen final — ce qui vous attend
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
WHAT IS ON IT 10 min
The final is 60 points and counts for 10% of your semester grade. Here is exactly what is in it.
  • Section A — Listening, 15 points. Fifteen questions, each with a clip you can replay as often as you like. Every clip comes from a passage you have already studied, drawn across all nine modules — nothing new.
  • Section B — Reading, 15 points. Two short texts: a café menu and a text-message exchange between two friends. Multiple choice and true/false.
  • Section C — Grammar, 18 points. Two items per module, so every grammar point of the semester appears exactly twice. Today’s review items come from the same nine modules.
  • Section D — Written production, 12 points. One prompt: describe a memorable day, using both the passé composé and the imparfait. Four to six sentences. Graded on a rubric, not by a machine.
Two attempts. The higher score counts, so the first one is not the one that decides anything. Accents are never required for credit — but don’t forget them; they are part of the word.

Défi éclair
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
BUILD IT YOURSELF 13 min
Four builds from across the semester. The last one is closest to the written task on the exam. Build each answer yourself: use the Mots utiles key if you need it, type your answer and press Check. The answer unlocks once you have tried.
⚡ DÉFI 1
In French: I’ll have the fish with vegetables.
Défi 1 · your answer
Accents are part of the word. Click in a box, then click a letter to add it.
Je prends du poisson avec des légumes.
du for the masculine noun, des for the plural.
⚡ DÉFI 2
Rewrite with a pronoun: J’offre une guitare à ma sœur.
Défi 2 · your answer
Accents are part of the word. Click in a box, then click a letter to add it.
Je lui offre une guitare.
lui replaces à + one person.
⚡ DÉFI 3
In French: I would like the least expensive dish, please.
Défi 3 · your answer
Accents are part of the word. Click in a box, then click a letter to add it.
Je voudrais le plat le moins cher, s’il vous plaît.
voudrais + a superlative with its article.
