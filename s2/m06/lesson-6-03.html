Les emplois de l’imparfait

What the Imparfait Is For · about 55 minutes
Le tableau du module : Hubert Robert, La Grande Galerie du Louvre (The Grande Galerie of the Louvre), c. 1795
TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you built the tense. Today you learn what it’s for — and it’s all about painting a scene.
The imparfait has three big jobs: it describes (how things looked and felt), it sets the background of a story, and it says what used to happen — the habits and repeated actions of the past. Then you’ll conjugate a full verb through all six persons — on your own, before the answer appears — and finish by typing imparfait forms the way the quizzes ask for them.
BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to name the three main uses of the imparfait: description, background, and habitual/repeated past.

IDÉCRIRE & PLANTER LE DÉCOR10 min

Use 1 & 2: description and background

Use 1 — Description. How things looked, felt, seemed: weather, mood, age, appearance, states of mind. Think of the imparfait as the brush that paints the scene.
Use 2 — Background. What was going on when something else happened — the ongoing setting behind an event.

Listen — every verb here paints, it doesn’t report:

  • Le musée était calme et magnifique.description — how it was
  • Il faisait beau et les rues étaient calmes.weather + setting
  • Il y avait beaucoup de tableaux.background — there were…
IICE QU'ON FAISAIT « AVANT »10 min

Use 3: the habitual / repeated past — “used to”

Use 3 — Habitual. Actions repeated over and over in the past — what you used to do. Watch for signal words: autrefois (formerly), d’habitude (usually), souvent (often), chaque (each), toujours (always).

English hides this behind “used to” or “would”; French just uses the imparfait. Listen:

  • Quand j’étais petit, je dessinais souvent.used to draw often
  • Nous allions au musée chaque dimanche.used to go every Sunday

🧠 USED TO · L’IMPARFAIT
When I was little (a girl speaking), I used to watch films.
Usually, we visited the museum.
In the past, my brother drew a lot.
Accents are part of the word. Click in a box, then click a letter to add it.
1. Quand j’étais petite, je regardais des films.When I was little (a girl speaking), I used to watch films.
2. D’habitude, nous visitions le musée.Usually, we visited the museum.
3. Autrefois, mon frère dessinait beaucoup.In the past, my brother drew a lot.

Three habits, three verbs in the imparfait: regardais, visitions, dessinait. A girl says petite; a boy says petit.
