Lesson 6.03
⚜ Les emplois de l’imparfait — What the Imparfait Is For
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you built the tense. Today you learn what it’s for — and it’s all about painting a scene.
The imparfait has three big jobs: it describes (how things looked and felt), it sets the background of a story, and it says what used to happen — the habits and repeated actions of the past. Then you’ll conjugate a full verb through all six persons — on your own, before the answer appears.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to name the three main uses of the imparfait: description, background, and habitual/repeated past.
2
I will be able to conjugate an imparfait verb through all six persons without help.
🎨 PART 1 · DÉCRIRE & PLANTER LE DÉCOR 12 min

Use 1 & 2: description and background

Use 1 — Description. How things looked, felt, seemed: weather, mood, age, appearance, states of mind. Think of the imparfait as the brush that paints the scene.
Use 2 — Background. What was going on when something else happened — the ongoing setting behind an event.

Listen — every verb here paints, it doesn’t report:

  • Le musée était calme et magnifique.description — how it was
  • Il faisait beau et les rues étaient calmes.weather + setting
  • Il y avait beaucoup de tableaux.background — there were…
🔄 PART 2 · CE QU'ON FAISAIT « AVANT » 10 min

Use 3: the habitual / repeated past — “used to”

Use 3 — Habitual. Actions repeated over and over in the past — what you used to do. Watch for signal words: autrefois (formerly), d’habitude (usually), souvent (often), chaque (each), toujours (always).

English hides this behind “used to” or “would”; French just uses the imparfait. Listen:

  • Quand j’étais petit, je dessinais souvent.used to draw often
  • Nous allions au musée chaque dimanche.used to go every Sunday
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Write three “quand j’étais petit(e)” sentences about what you used to do — a favorite show, a place you visited, a hobby. Every verb should be in the imparfait. This is the exact sentence shape you’ll speak on Day 8.
📋 PART 3 · LE TABLEAU COMPLET — ADMIRER 10 min

All six persons, one verb

This is the model for every regular -er verb in the imparfait. Stem = nous admirons → admir-. Read each row aloud.

PersonFormMeaning
jej’admiraisI was admiring / used to admire
tutu admiraisyou were admiring
il / elle / onil admiraithe / she / one was admiring
nousnous admirionswe were admiring
vousvous admiriezyou (formal/plural) were admiring
ils / ellesils admiraientthey were admiring
✍️ PART 4 · À TON TOUR 8 min

Now conjugate dessiner (to draw) in the imparfait, through all six persons — on paper or in your head. Commit to an answer first, then reveal to check. Trying before you see the answer is what makes it stick.

🧠 CONJUGATE · dessiner (imparfait)
Write all six imparfait forms of dessiner, then reveal.
je dessinaisnous dessinions
tu dessinaisvous dessiniez
il/elle/on dessinaitils/elles dessinaient
Same endings as admirer — only the stem changed (dessin-). If you matched all six, you’ve got imparfait formation cold.
