⚜ Au musée — The Language of Art
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Step inside a French museum — and pick up the words for what hangs on its walls.
This module lives in the world of art and museums. Today you meet the first ten words — the painting, the painter, the sculpture, the masterpiece — and you hear each one spoken before you try to say it. This week also opens a big new idea: a second past tense, the imparfait, the tense that paints a memory instead of just reporting it.
🔄 UN PETIT RETOUR · A LOOK BACK

You can already say what happened with the passé composé: j’ai visité le musée (I visited the museum). The imparfait is its partner — it paints the scene, the mood, and what you used to do. By Friday you’ll read a whole museum memory that uses both.

🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce ten French words for art and museums.
2
I will be able to match each spoken art word to its meaning by ear.

⏰ PART 1 · ENTRONS AU MUSÉE 12 min

First, a question about memory

France gave the world some of its most famous art — the Louvre, the Mona Lisa, the Impressionists. But art is also personal: a painting you loved as a child, a museum you used to visit. This module is about both. Let’s start with you.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
When you think of “a museum,” what comes to mind first?
📚 PART 2 · LE VOCABULAIRE DE L'ART 18 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 on each word and say it back out loud. Notice the little words in front — le, la, l’: French nouns carry their gender everywhere. Get the article with the word in your ear from day one.

  • le tableauthe painting
  • la peinturepainting (the art form)
  • le peintrethe painter
  • l’artistethe artist
  • la sculpturethe sculpture
  • l’expositionthe exhibition
  • le chef-d’œuvrethe masterpiece
  • le filmthe film
  • l’acteurthe actor
  • l’actricethe actress
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Go down the list a second time, but this time shadow each word: play it, pause, and repeat it out loud three times before moving on. Notice the pair le peintre (the painter) vs la peinture (painting) — one letter of difference, two meanings.
📝 PART 3 · RÉVISE TON VOCABULAIRE 10 min

Drill the words with the Vocabulaire page

You don’t have to build your word bank from scratch. Open the Module 6 Vocabulaire study page (in this module) — every word, its meaning, and its audio in one place, as flip cards you can drill by ear. Because your Vocabulary Check is listening-based, practice by hearing the word and recalling its meaning.

Making your own paper or digital cards on top of that is a great memory booster — but it’s optional, not required.
