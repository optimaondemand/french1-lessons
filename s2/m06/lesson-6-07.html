📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Sharpen the sound of the imparfait, then speak — a memory of your own, out loud, on the record.
First a quick clinic on the sound at the heart of this module: the imparfait endings -ais, -ait, -aient all say the same thing to your ear. Then you’ll record your Speaking Task — the museum or art memory you’ve been building since Day 4, mixing the imparfait and the passé composé. You’ll finish by running the imparfait of être from memory, ready for tomorrow’s Grammar Quiz.
🎤 PART 1 · PHONÉTIQUE 13 min

The three endings that sound alike

-ais, -ait, and -aient are all pronounced the same — one open “eh” sound, [ε]. So je regardais, il regardait, and ils regardaient sound identical; only the spelling (and the subject) tells them apart. The final consonants are silent.
  • …était calme et magnifique.-ait = “eh”
  • …je dessinais souvent.-ais = same “eh”
  • Je regardais un tableau…-ais again
But -ions and -iez are different. The nous and vous forms add a clear “ee” glide before the ending: nous all-ions, vous all-iez. Don’t flatten them into the “eh” sound.
  • Nous allions au musée…-ions = “ee-oh”

Shadow it: play each clip, pause, and copy the exact vowel three times. Aim to match the open “eh” of the singular endings and the “ee” glide of -ions.

🎤 Speaking Task · Un souvenir d’art (An art memory)
15 pts · 1 submission
SUBMITTED IN CANVAS · MEDIA RECORDING · should be submitted by the end of the module 15 min
  • Record 6–8 sentences describing a memory tied to art, a museum, a movie, or a place you used to go.
  • Use at least three imparfait verbs (description / habit: c’était, il y avait, j’allais…) and at least two passé composé verbs (events: un jour, j’ai…).
  • Include at least two Module 6 art words (le tableau, la salle, magnifique…).
  • You are encouraged to prepare a script to help you get ready for your recording. This is your Day 4 / Day 5 writing, spoken aloud.

⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +8 min
Record-and-compare: play paragraph 2 of Une visite au musée from Lesson 6.05, then record yourself saying the same lines. Listen back-to-back and note one sound to fix. Imitating a native model is the fastest route to a French accent.
📋 PART 3 · LE TABLEAU, ENCORE UNE FOIS 13 min

From memory, conjugate être in the imparfait through all six persons — it’s the one irregular stem (ét-), and you’ll use it constantly for description. Commit first — then reveal.

🧠 CONJUGATE · être (imparfait)
j’étaisnous étions
tu étaisvous étiez
il/elle/on étaitils/elles étaient
Same six endings as every verb — only the stem (ét-) is irregular. If all six matched, you’re ready for tomorrow’s Grammar Quiz.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +10 min · cumulative spiral
Spiral back to Module 5: take two reflexive verbs you learned (se lever, se promener) and put them into the imparfait — quand j’étais petit, je me levais tard le dimanche et nous nous promenions au parc. Reflexive + imparfait together is a common Grammar Quiz trap; practice it now.
