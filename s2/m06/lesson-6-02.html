⚜ Décrire & l’imparfait — Describing + Building the Imparfait
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Ten words for describing art and the past — then the machine that builds the imparfait.
First you pick up the describing words (magnifique, calme, impressionnant) and the time words that signal the past (autrefois, d’habitude). Then you meet a brand-new tense: the imparfait. Today is pure formation — how to build it. One stem, one set of endings, and a couple of high-frequency shortcuts (était, avait, il y avait).
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce the describing and time words (words 11–20).
2
I will be able to form the imparfait from the nous-stem and add the six endings, including était and il y avait.

🧠 PART 2 · LA GRAMMAIRE — FORMER L'IMPARFAIT 17 min

One stem, one set of endings

The imparfait is the easiest tense to build in all of French, because it’s almost completely regular. Here is the whole recipe:

Step 1. Take the nous form of the present and drop the -ons. That’s your stem.
nous regardons → regard-  ·  nous finissons → finiss-  ·  nous allons → all-
Step 2. Add the endings: -ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient.
SubjectEndingExample: regarder (stem regard-)
je-aisje regardais — I was looking / used to look
tu-aistu regardais
il / elle / on-aitil regardait
nous-ionsnous regardions
vous-iezvous regardiez
ils / elles-aientils regardaient
One exception + two must-knows. Only être is irregular — its stem is ét-: j’étais, il était, nous étions. From avoir (stem av-) you get il avait. And the single most useful imparfait phrase for describing a scene: il y avait = “there was / there were.”

Hear the forms you’ll use most. Listen for the -ait and -ais endings — they sound the same:

  • Le musée était calme et magnifique.was — être, imparfait
  • Il y avait beaucoup de tableaux.there were — il y avait
  • Quand j’étais petit, je dessinais souvent.was / used to draw
  • Nous allions au musée chaque dimanche.we used to go — nous -ions
