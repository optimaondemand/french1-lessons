Lesson 6.04
⚜ Passé composé vs. imparfait — Event vs. Scene
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
The heart of the module: when to use which past tense. One paints the scene; the other reports what happened in it.
You already know the passé composé (completed actions — j’ai visité) and now the imparfait (ongoing scenes — c’était). French stories almost always use them together: the imparfait sets the stage, and the passé composé is the thing that happened. Today you learn to choose.
⚠️ Gate-check focus. Choosing correctly between the passé composé and the imparfait is the exact skill your Day-10 Grammar Quiz (the gate check) measures. You’ll need 80% to move on to Module 7 without a review plan. Everything today is aimed straight at that.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to choose the passé composé for completed events and the imparfait for ongoing scenes/description.
2
I will be able to understand a spoken sentence that mixes both tenses and write it down.
⚖️ LE CONTRASTE 10 min
Use the IMPARFAIT when…Use the PASSÉ COMPOSÉ when…
you describe a scene, mood, or state
Le musée était calme.		a single event was completed
J’ai visité le Louvre.
it’s the background — what was going on
Je regardais un tableau…		it’s the interruption — what happened
…quand mon ami est arrivé.
it’s a habit / repeated (“used to”)
Nous allions au musée chaque dimanche.		it happened once, at a specific time
Hier, nous sommes allés au musée.

The shortcut: if you could add “was ___-ing” or “used to” in English, it’s the imparfait. If it’s a one-time, finished “I did / it happened,” it’s the passé composé.

🎧 LES DEUX ENSEMBLE 6 min

Hear both tenses working together. In each clip, notice the scene and the event:

Je regardais un tableau quand mon ami est arrivé. — I was looking at a painting when my friend arrived.
Hier, j’ai visité le Louvre ; c’était magnifique. — Yesterday I visited the Louvre; it was magnificent.
✅ CHOISIS LE BON TEMPS 10 min

Four sentences. Pick the tense the meaning calls for. Instant feedback — practice, not a grade.

✎ ITEM 1
“The museum was calm and magnificent.” (a description)
✎ ITEM 2
“Yesterday, I visited the Louvre.” (once, finished, hier)
✎ ITEM 3
“When I was little, I used to draw often.” (a repeated habit)
✎ ITEM 4
“I was looking at a painting when my friend arrived.” Which pairing is right?
✍️ À TOI D'ÉCRIRE 8 min

Write three sentences that mix both tenses

In your notebook, write three sentences about a real visit — a museum, a trip, a movie. Each should have a scene in the imparfait and an event in the passé composé.

Model: Le musée était calme et il y avait un grand tableau. Je regardais ce tableau quand un guide a commencé à parler. J’ai admiré les couleurs : elles étaient magnifiques.

Keep these — you’ll reuse them for your Speaking Task on Day 8.

🎧 LA DICTÉE 10 min

Hear it, write it, check it

Play each clip (as many times as you need). Write down exactly what you hear — accents and all — then reveal to check. Don’t reveal early; the struggle is the learning.

1A description — listen for était.
Le musée était calme et magnifique.
2Background — the “there were” phrase.
Il y avait beaucoup de tableaux.
3Both tenses — scene + event.
Je regardais un tableau quand mon ami est arrivé.
4Event + state, in one sentence.
Hier, j’ai visité le Louvre ; c’était magnifique.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +8 min
Spiral back to Module 4/5: take a story you can already tell in the passé composé and add two imparfait sentences of scene-setting to the front (weather, mood, what was around). Watch how much richer it becomes — that layering is exactly what the gate check rewards.
