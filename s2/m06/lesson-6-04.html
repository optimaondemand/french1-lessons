Passé composé vs. imparfait

Event vs. Scene · about 50 minutes
Le tableau du module : Hubert Robert, La Grande Galerie du Louvre (The Grande Galerie of the Louvre), c. 1795
📌 Module 6 has four graded assessments to complete before you move on to Module 7: Vocabulary CheckLangue en actionSpeaking TaskGrammar QuizYou take them on Days 6–8, and you can tick each one off there once it is submitted.
TODAY'S FOCUS
The heart of the module: when to use which past tense. One paints the scene; the other reports what happened in it.
You already know the passé composé (completed actions — j’ai visité) and now the imparfait (ongoing scenes — c’était). French stories almost always use them together: the imparfait sets the stage, and the passé composé is the thing that happened. Today you learn to choose — and you finish with seven quick practice items that mix imparfait formation with the tense choice.
⚠️ Grammar Quiz focus. Choosing correctly between the passé composé and the imparfait is the exact skill your Day-8 Grammar Quiz measures. Everything today is aimed straight at that.
BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to choose the passé composé for completed events and the imparfait for ongoing scenes/description.
2
I will be able to understand a spoken sentence that mixes both tenses and write it down.
3
I will be able to form the imparfait and choose between the two past tenses in short practice items.
LE CONTRASTE 10 min
Use the IMPARFAIT when…Use the PASSÉ COMPOSÉ when…
you describe a scene, mood, or state
Le musée était calme.		a single event was completed
J’ai visité le Louvre.
it’s the background — what was going on
Je regardais un tableau…		it’s the interruption — what happened
…quand mon ami est arrivé.
it’s a habit / repeated (“used to”)
Nous allions au musée chaque dimanche.		it happened once, at a specific time
Hier, nous sommes allés au musée.

The shortcut: if you could add “was ___-ing” or “used to” in English, it’s the imparfait. If it’s a one-time, finished “I did / it happened,” it’s the passé composé.

LES DEUX ENSEMBLE 6 min

Hear both tenses working together. In each clip, notice the scene and the event:

Je regardais un tableau quand mon ami est arrivé. — I was looking at a painting when my friend arrived.
Hier, j’ai visité le Louvre ; c’était magnifique. — Yesterday I visited the Louvre; it was magnificent.
CHOISIS LE BON TEMPS 10 min

Four sentences. Pick the tense the meaning calls for. Instant feedback — practice, not a grade.

✎ ITEM 1
“The museum was calm and magnificent.” (a description)
✎ ITEM 2
“Yesterday, I visited the Louvre.” (once, finished, hier)
✎ ITEM 3
“When I was little, I used to draw often.” (a repeated habit)
✎ ITEM 4
“I was looking at a painting when my friend arrived.” Which pairing is right?
À TOI D'ÉCRIRE 8 min

Write three sentences that mix both tenses

In your notebook, write three sentences about a real visit — a museum, a trip, a movie. Each should have a scene in the imparfait and an event in the passé composé.

Model: Le musée était calme et il y avait un grand tableau. Je regardais ce tableau quand un guide a commencé à parler. J’ai admiré les couleurs : elles étaient magnifiques. (un guide (a museum guide) · les couleurs (the colors) · a commencé à parler (began to speak))

Keep these — you’ll reuse them for your Speaking Task on Day 7.

LA DICTÉE 10 min

Hear it, type it, check it

Play each clip (as many times as you need). Type exactly what you hear — accents and all — then press Check. Use Reveal only after you have tried. Don’t reveal early; the struggle is the learning.

Accents: — click a box first, then a letter.
1A description — listen for était.
Le musée était calme et magnifique.
2Background — the “there were” phrase.
Il y avait beaucoup de tableaux.
3Both tenses — scene + event.
Je regardais un tableau quand mon ami est arrivé.
4Event + state, in one sentence.
Hier, j’ai visité le Louvre ; c’était magnifique.

IMPARFAIT vs PASSÉ COMPOSÉ 12 min
Seven items on Module 6’s grammar: forming the imparfait, and choosing between the two past tenses.
✎ ITEM 1
Quand j’étais petit, j’______ souvent au musée. (aller)
✎ ITEM 2
Nous ______ beaucoup de temps au musée. (avoir, imparfait)
✎ ITEM 3
Il ______ très calme dans la salle. (être, imparfait)
✎ ITEM 4
Which tense describes background — weather, feelings, scenery?
✎ ITEM 5
Hier, je ______ le tableau. (voir — one completed action)
✎ ITEM 6
Elle ______ au musée le samedi. (aller — a habit)
✎ ITEM 7
Les tableaux ______ magnifiques. (être — description)
