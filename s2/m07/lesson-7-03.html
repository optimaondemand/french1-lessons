Lesson 7.03
⚜ Le COD au passé — « Je l'ai achetée »
Lesson · about 50 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday, the present. Today, the past — where the pronoun does one surprising thing to the verb behind it.
When you talk about shopping you did, you use the passé composé (you met it in Module 6): j'ai acheté. Put a direct object pronoun in and it jumps in front of ai/as/a: je l'ai acheté. And here's Rule 2: when that pronoun is feminine or plural, the past participle agrees with it — la robe… je l'ai achetée. You'll finish with a quick Défi éclair: three sentences to build in your head before you reveal them.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to place the direct object pronoun before the auxiliary in the passé composé (je l'ai vu).
2
I will be able to make the past participle agree with a preceding feminine or plural object (je l'ai achetée, je les ai achetées).
3
I will be able to build short sentences with a pronoun in the present and the past, without looking.
⏳ PART 1 · OÙ VA LE PRONOM AU PASSÉ ? 11 min

The pronoun goes before ai / as / a

Rule 1 (past). In the passé composé, the direct object pronoun sits right before the auxiliary (avoir): J'ai vu la jupe → je l'ai vue. The pronoun and ai stay glued together.

Listen to the model sentence — it does both moves at once (pronoun before ai, and the agreement you'll meet next):

  • J'ai vu la jupe et je l'ai achetée.I saw the skirt and I bought it.
✍️ PART 2 · RÈGLE N°2 — L'ACCORD DU PARTICIPE 12 min

The past participle grows to match

Rule 2. When a direct object pronoun comes before the verb, the past participle agrees with it in gender and number — just like an adjective. Add -e for feminine, -s for plural, -es for feminine plural.
The nounPronounPast participleFull sentence
le pull (m. sing.)l'achetéJe l'ai acheté.
la robe (f. sing.)l'achetéeJe l'ai achetée.
les jeans (m. pl.)lesachetésJe les ai achetés.
les chaussures (f. pl.)lesachetéesJe les ai achetées.

Good news for your ear: with -er verbs like acheter, the extra -e and -s are silent — acheté and achetée sound identical. Agreement is mostly a writing rule. (The exception is vue vs vu — still silent here too.)

⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Spiral back to Module 6: there you built the passé composé with avoir + past participle. Write two sentences with a normal object (j'ai acheté une robe) and then rewrite each replacing the noun with a pronoun (je l'ai achetée). Watch the participle change.

✍️ PART 4 · À TON TOUR 8 min

Rewrite each sentence, replacing the underlined noun with a pronoun — and fix the participle if needed. Commit to an answer first, then reveal to check.

🧠 TRANSFORME · noun → pronoun (past)
1. J'ai acheté la robe.    2. J'ai vu les vitrines.    3. J'ai essayé le manteau.
1.Je l'ai achetée.la robe → f. sing. → +e
2.Je les ai vues.les vitrines → f. pl. → +es
3.Je l'ai essayé.le manteau → m. sing. → no change
If you got the pronoun right and the participle ending right, you've got both rules cold.
