📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Sharpen the sound, then speak — a shopping trip, out loud, on the record.
First a quick clinic on two sounds at the heart of this module: the elided l' that glides into the next word, and the -oy- in voir. Then you'll record your Speaking Task — the shopping story you've been building since Day 4. You'll finish by running voir from memory, ready for tomorrow's Grammar Quiz.
🎤 PART 1 · PHONÉTIQUE 13 min

The elided l' and the sounds of le / la / les

Before a consonant, the pronoun keeps its own sound — a light, quick le, la, or les. Keep it short and unstressed; don't lean on it.
  • je le portele + consonant (p)
  • je la trouvela + consonant (t)
  • je les achèteles + vowel → liaison « z »
Before a vowel, le and la drop their vowel and glide into the next word as l' — no pause: je l'essaie sounds like one word. And listen for the -oy- of voir, which sounds like “vwa-y”.
  • je l'essaiel' + vowel (e)
  • nous voyonsthe -oy- of voir

Shadow it: play each clip, pause, and copy the exact rhythm three times. Aim to match the glide of l', not just the words.

🎤 Speaking Task · Une journée de shopping
15 pts · 1 submission
SUBMITTED IN CANVAS · MEDIA RECORDING · should be submitted by the end of the module 15 min
  • Record 6–8 sentences describing a shopping trip — real or imagined.
  • Use at least five clothing/shopping words, at least three direct object pronouns (le, la, l', les), and one sentence in the past (je l'ai achetée).
  • You are encouraged to prepare a script to help you get ready for your recording.
  • This is your Day 4 / Day 5 writing, spoken aloud. You've already built it.

✅ PART 2 · AVANT D'ENVOYER 12 min

Record once, listen back, and check yourself against this list. You may re-record before you submit — use that buffer.

□ I said 6–8 sentences.
□ Every pronoun matches its noun (gender & number) and sits before the verb.
□ My elisions glide (je l'essaie, not “je luh essaie”).
□ My past sentence has the participle agreement (je l'ai achetée).
□ I used at least five clothing/shopping words.
□ I can hear my own words clearly on playback.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +8 min
Record-and-compare: play Julie's paragraph 2 from Lesson 7.05, then record yourself saying the same lines. Listen back-to-back and note one sound to fix. Imitation of a native model is the fastest route to a French accent.
📋 PART 3 · LE VERBE VOIR, ENCORE UNE FOIS 13 min

From memory, conjugate voir through all six persons. Commit first — then reveal.

🧠 CONJUGATE · voir
je voisnous voyons
tu voisvous voyez
il/elle/on voitils/elles voient
Remember the -oy- in nous voyons and vous voyez. If all six matched, you're ready for tomorrow's Grammar Quiz.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +10 min · cumulative spiral
Mix Module 7 with earlier modules: write four sentences that each combine a clothing word with vocabulary you already know — a color (Lesson 7.02), a family member (Module 3), a leisure activity (Module 6), or the passé composé (Module 6). Interleaving old and new is what makes review actually stick.
