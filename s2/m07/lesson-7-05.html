⚜ Une journée de shopping — Reading & Culture
Lesson · about 50 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
See every word and pronoun from Week 1 living inside one real shopping trip — and hear it read aloud.
Julie is shopping for a party dress. Her afternoon uses your vocabulary, your pronouns, and voir — all at once. Read and listen, then step back to see why Paris is the world's fashion capital, and meet a woman whose courage rewrote how the whole world dresses. You'll finish by writing a shopping story of your own — your rehearsal for tomorrow's graded work and the Speaking Task.
📖 PART 1 · LA LECTURE 15 min

Read each paragraph, then press ▶ Écouter and follow along with your eyes. Read it a second time with the audio playing — that pairing of eye and ear is where fluency starts.

A few words to help the passage read smoothly — look them over before you read:
  • faire du shopping — to go shopping
  • chercher — to look for
  • pour — for
  • une fête — a party
  • bleue — blue (f.)
  • parfaite — perfect (f.)
  • rouge — red
  • alors — so / therefore
  • adorer — to love / adore

J’adore faire du shopping. Aujourd’hui, je cherche une robe pour une fête.

Je vois une belle robe bleue dans la vitrine. Je l’essaie : la taille est parfaite !

Le pull rouge est très cher, alors je ne l’achète pas. Mais les chaussures ? Je les adore, et je les achète !

🖌️ PART 2 · ANNOTE LE TEXTE 8 min

On a printout or in your notes, circle every direct object pronoun (le, la, l', les) and draw an arrow to the noun each one replaces. You should find four. Notice the negative one — je ne l'achète pas — and how the pronoun still sits right before the verb, inside the ne…pas.

🇫🇷 PART 3 · PARIS, CAPITALE DE LA MODE 12 min

Paris is one of the world's great fashion capitals. In the 1920s, Coco Chanel freed women from the stiff, corseted styles of the past — she used soft jersey fabric and gave the world la petite robe noire, the little black dress that never goes out of style. In 1947, Christian Dior's “New Look” revived French haute couture (high fashion) with sweeping, elegant lines after the hardship of war.

Twice a year, la Semaine de la mode (Paris Fashion Week) still draws designers, buyers, and photographers from every continent. When you learn the vocabulary of la mode, you're stepping into a conversation France has led for a hundred years.

⚜ PART 4 · UNE VERTU FRANÇAISE 5 min
LE COURAGE · COURAGE

Coco Chanel (1883–1971) grew up in an orphanage with nothing. To design clothes that let women move, breathe, and work — trousers, soft knits, the simple black dress — she had to defy a whole industry that insisted fashion meant discomfort. It took courage to tell the world it was wrong.

Style isn't about following the crowd — it's the courage to be yourself. « Pour être irremplaçable, il faut être différent. » — “To be irreplaceable, one must be different.”

⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min · comprehension check
Answer from the passage. Instant feedback.
Qu'est-ce que Julie cherche aujourd'hui ?
Pourquoi est-ce que Julie n'achète pas le pull rouge ?
Qu'est-ce que Julie achète à la fin ?
✍️ PART 5 · TON PROPRE SHOPPING 15 min

Write five sentences about a shopping trip

Tell the story of a real or imagined shopping trip. Use at least five clothing/shopping words, at least three direct object pronouns (le, la, l', les), one negative (je ne l'achète pas), and one sentence in the past (je l'ai achetée). This is your rehearsal for both the Speaking Task and Langue en action — write it well and reuse it.

Checklist before you stop:  □ a pronoun matches its noun's gender/number  □ the pronoun sits before the verb  □ elision before a vowel (je l'essaie)  □ a past sentence with participle agreement  □ a price word (cher / bon marché).
