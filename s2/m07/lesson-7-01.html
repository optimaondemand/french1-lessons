📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
What you wear says something about who you are — so how do you say it in French?
Welcome to the module the French take more seriously than anyone: la mode. This week you'll shop, describe, and compare clothes — and learn a grammar trick that stops you repeating the same noun over and over. Today you meet ten pieces of clothing. You will hear each one spoken before you ever try to say it.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce ten French clothing words with their gender (le / la / les).
2
I will be able to match each spoken clothing word to its meaning by ear.
📋 WHAT YOU NEED FOR THIS LESSON
▶
Headphones or speakers — today is a listening day.
▶
Your vocabulary notebook or flashcard app.
⏰ PART 1 · SE METTRE EN ROUTE 12 min

First, a quick look back

You already know French nouns come with a gender: le for masculine, la for feminine, les for plural. Clothing is no different. As each word appears, notice the little article in front — it's part of the word, and later this week it becomes the key to a grammar shortcut.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
How would you describe your own everyday style?
📚 PART 2 · LE VOCABULAIRE DES VÊTEMENTS 18 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 on each word and say it back out loud. Learn the article with the word — is it le, la, or les? Knowing the gender now will make everything else this module easier.

  • les vêtementsclothes
  • la chemisethe shirt
  • le pantalonthe pants
  • la robethe dress
  • la jupethe skirt
  • le manteauthe coat
  • les chaussuresthe shoes
  • le chapeauthe hat
  • le pullthe sweater
  • le jeanthe jeans
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Go down the list a second time, but this time shadow each word: play it, pause, and repeat it out loud three times before moving on. Say the article too (la robe, not just robe) — the gender should feel glued to the noun.
📝 PART 3 · RÉVISE TON VOCABULAIRE 10 min

Drill the words with the Vocabulaire page

You don’t have to build your word bank from scratch. Open the Module 7 Vocabulaire study page (in this module) — every word, its meaning, and its audio in one place, as flip cards you can drill by ear. Because your Vocabulary Check is listening-based, practice by hearing the word and recalling its meaning.

Making your own paper or digital cards on top of that is a great memory booster — but it’s optional, not required.
