Lesson 7.04
⚜ Voir & mise en pratique — See It, Say It
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
One more verb — then you put everything to work in a real shop, and write down French by ear.
Shopping starts with seeing: je vois une robe dans la vitrine. Voir (to see) is irregular, so you learn it as its own little table. Then three moves: choose the right pronoun, write your own shopping sentences, and take an audio dictation — the bridge between hearing French and producing it.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to conjugate voir through all six persons and use pronouns in a shopping scene.
2
I will be able to understand a spoken sentence with a pronoun well enough to write it down.
👀 PART 1 · LE VERBE VOIR 10 min

To see — an irregular verb worth knowing

Notice the -oy- spelling in the nous and vous forms — that's the tricky part. Read the table aloud, then hear the two you'll use most.

PersonFormMeaning
jeje voisI see
tutu voisyou see
il / elle / onil voithe / she / one sees
nousnous voyonswe see
vousvous voyezyou (formal/plural) see
ils / ellesils voientthey see
  • Je vois une belle vitrine.I see a beautiful shop window.
  • Nous voyons beaucoup de vêtements.We see a lot of clothes. (nous form)
✅ PART 2 · AU MAGASIN — CHOISIS BIEN 10 min

You're in the shop. Pick the correct form. Instant feedback — practice, not a grade.

✎ ITEM 1
Nous ______ une jolie vitrine. (voir)
✎ ITEM 2
Ce pull est joli. Je ______ achète. (present, m. sing.)
✎ ITEM 3
La robe bleue ? Je l'ai ______ hier. (passé, agreement!)
✎ ITEM 4
Les chaussures ? Je les ai ______ au magasin. (passé, f. pl.)
✍️ PART 3 · À TOI D'ÉCRIRE 8 min

Write three sentences about a shopping trip

In your notebook, write three sentences about clothes you like or bought. Use a direct object pronoun in each (le, la, l', les), and make at least one in the past (je l'ai achetée).

Model: Je vois une belle robe dans la vitrine. Je la trouve jolie, alors je l'essaie. La taille est parfaite — je l'ai achetée !

Keep these — you'll reuse them for your Speaking Task on Day 8. Check each sentence: right pronoun, right place, participle agreement in the past.

🎧 PART 4 · LA DICTÉE 12 min

Hear it, write it, check it

Play each clip (as many times as you need). Write down exactly what you hear — accents and all — then reveal to check. Don't reveal early; the struggle is the learning.

1Play, write, then reveal.
J'aime ce pull. Je le porte souvent.
2Play, write, then reveal.
Cette robe ? Je la trouve jolie.
3Listen for the elision (l').
Ce manteau, je l'essaie.
4A past sentence — mind the agreement.
J'ai vu la jupe et je l'ai achetée.
5The nous form of voir.
Nous voyons beaucoup de vêtements dans ce magasin.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +10 min
Spiral to Module 6 (the passé composé): write four past sentences about a shopping trip, each with a direct object pronoun. Getting the participle agreement right in the past is the single most common slip — practice it now, while it's fresh.
