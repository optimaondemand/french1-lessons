Lesson 7.02
⚜ Le shopping & le pronom COD — Say It Without Repeating
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you named the clothes. Today you go shopping — and learn the word that replaces the noun so you don't say it twice.
First, ten shopping words: buying, trying on, sizes, prices. Then the module's big idea — the direct object pronoun (le, la, l', les). Instead of “I like the sweater, I wear the sweater,” French says “I like the sweater, I wear it” — je le porte. Rule 1: the pronoun goes before the verb.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce the shopping words (words 11–20).
2
I will be able to choose le / la / l' / les to match a noun's gender and number, and place it before the verb.

🧠 PART 2 · LA GRAMMAIRE — LE PRONOM COD 17 min

A word that replaces the thing

A direct object pronoun (in French, le pronom COD) stands in for a noun you've already named, so you don't repeat it. English does this too: “I see the dress — I love it.” The French pronoun you choose must match the gender and number of the noun it replaces, and — here is Rule 1 — it goes before the verb, not after.

Replaces…PronounExample
masculine nounleLe pull ? Je le porte.
feminine nounlaLa robe ? Je la trouve jolie.
before a vowel (m. or f.)l'Le manteau ? Je l'essaie.
plural nounlesLes chaussures ? Je les achète.

Hear all four. Notice the pronoun always lands right before the verb:

  • Je le porte souvent.I wear it often. (le pull)
  • Je la trouve jolie.I find it pretty. (la robe)
  • Je l'essaie.I'm trying it on. (le manteau)
  • Je les achète.I'm buying them. (les chaussures)
