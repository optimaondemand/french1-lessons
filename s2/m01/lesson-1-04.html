Le verbe faire & Mise en pratique

TODAY'S FOCUS
One verb does a hundred jobs in French: faire. Learn it, then use everything — adjectives, BANGS, and faire — to describe your own family.
Faire means "to do" or "to make," but the French reach for it everywhere: faire les devoirs (to do homework), faire une promenade (to go for a walk), faire la cuisine (to cook). It's irregular, so today you memorize its six forms, then write about your family and listen to a dictation and write down what you hear. You'll close with two short practice rounds that mix agreement, the irregulars, BANGS and faire.
BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…

2

3
I will be able to choose the correct adjective form, its place, and the right form of faire in a sentence.
LE VERBE FAIRE — TOUTES LES FORMES

Six forms — watch vous and ils

Most forms follow a pattern, but vous faites and ils/elles font are the two everyone gets wrong. Say the table aloud, then hear two forms in context.

PersonFormMeaning
jeje faisI do / make
tutu faisyou do / make
il / elle / onil faithe / she / one does
nousnous faisonswe do / make
vousvous faitesyou (formal/plural) do
ils / ellesils fontthey do / make
  • Je fais mes devoirs le soir.je fais
  • Nous faisons une promenade ensemble.nous faisons · ensemble (together)

Glossaire : la benjamine (the youngest child, f.) · un peu (a little)

The coach ticks each rule as you write and suggests a fix when an ending or a placement looks wrong. Keep these sentences: you will reuse them for your Speaking Task on Day 7.

⚡ DÉFI 1
Say it in French: My big brother is serious and hard-working.
My big brother is serious and hard-working.
Accents are part of the word. Click in a box, then click a letter to add it.
Mon grand frère est sérieux et travailleur.
grand comes before the noun (BANGS); the two adjectives after it stay masculine.
⚡ DÉFI 2
Make it feminine: Il est curieux et généreux.
Make it feminine: Il est curieux et généreux.
Elle est curieuse et généreuse.
Both adjectives end in -eux, so both become -euse.
⚡ DÉFI 3
Place the adjective: (beau) … une maison
(beau) … une maison
une belle maison
beau is a BANGS adjective (Beauty), so it goes before the noun — and agrees: belle.
