⚜ Le caractère — Personality Words
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Welcome to Semester 2. Last term you learned to name the people in your family — this module, you learn to describe them.
A family isn't just a list of names — it's a set of personalities. Today you meet ten words for character: kind, funny, serious, shy, hard-working. You will hear each one spoken before you try to say it, because a word you can hear is a word you can use.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce ten French adjectives that describe personality.
2
I will be able to match each spoken adjective to its meaning by ear.
📋 WHAT YOU NEED FOR THIS LESSON
▶
Headphones or speakers — today is a listening day.
▶
Your vocabulary notebook or flashcard app.
⏰ PART 1 · ON REPREND 12 min

First, a quick look back

In Semester 1 you learned to name the members of a family: le père, la mère, le frère, la sœur. You will lean on all of that this week — but now you'll say what each person is like. Warm up by naming three people in your family out loud in French.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Which word do you most want to be able to say about someone you love?
📚 PART 2 · LE VOCABULAIRE DU CARACTÈRE 18 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 on each word and say it back out loud. These are adjectives — describing words. A few change spelling for a woman (you'll see gentil / gentille); you'll learn how that works soon. Today, just get the sounds in your ear.

  • le caractèrecharacter / personality
  • gentilkind (f. gentille)
  • méchantmean / nasty
  • timideshy
  • sympathiquenice / friendly
  • sérieuxserious
  • drôlefunny
  • paresseuxlazy
  • travailleurhard-working
  • intelligentintelligent
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Go down the list a second time, but this time shadow each word: play it, pause, and repeat it out loud three times before moving on. Then say each one in a mini-sentence: Mon frère est… Shadowing is the single fastest way to make new sounds feel natural.
📝 PART 3 · RÉVISE TON VOCABULAIRE 10 min

Drill the words with the Vocabulaire page

You don’t have to build your word bank from scratch. Open the Module 1 Vocabulaire study page (in this module) — every word, its meaning, and its audio in one place, as flip cards you can drill by ear. Because your Vocabulary Check is listening-based, practice by hearing the word and recalling its meaning.

Making your own paper or digital cards on top of that is a great memory booster — but it’s optional, not required.

➡️ WHAT YOU'LL DO NEXT

Continue to Lesson 1.02

💪 ON EST PARTI(E)S !

You now have ten words to describe the people you know best. Say one of them out loud right now, from memory, about someone in your family. That’s how a word becomes yours — à demain !
