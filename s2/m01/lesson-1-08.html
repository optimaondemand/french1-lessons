📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Sharpen the sound, then speak — your own family, out loud, on the record.
First a quick clinic on the sound at the heart of this module: how an adjective changes when it describes a woman. Many masculine forms end in a silent letter that the feminine form pronounces. Then you'll record your Speaking Task — the family portrait you've been building all week.
🎤 PART 1 · PHONÉTIQUE 13 min

Masculine vs. feminine — hear the ending change

The masculine form often ends in a sound that stops short — beau, nouveau, vieux. The final consonant, if there is one, is usually silent.
  • beaumasculine — ends open
  • vieuxmasculine — the x is silent
  • nouveaumasculine — ends on the vowel
The feminine form adds an ending you can hear — a crisp consonant that the masculine kept hidden: belle, vieille, nouvelle. Listen for that extra sound at the end.
  • Ma sœur est belle et gentille.belle — the "l" is pronounced
  • C’est une vieille maison.vieille — the "y" sound appears
  • Nous avons un nouvel appartement.nouvel — the "l" links into the vowel

Shadow it: play each clip, pause, and copy the exact ending three times. Aim to make the feminine consonant land clearly — that's what tells a listener you agreed the adjective.

🎤 Speaking Task · Ma famille
15 pts · 1 submission
SUBMITTED IN CANVAS · MEDIA RECORDING 15 min
  • Record 6–8 sentences introducing your family and describing what each person is like.
  • Use at least five adjectives (with correct m./f. agreement), one irregular adjective (beau / nouveau / vieux), one correctly placed BANGS adjective, and the verb faire once.
  • Speak from notes, not a script read word-for-word — natural delivery counts.
  • This is your Day 4 / Day 6 writing, spoken aloud. You've already built it.

How it's scored (15 pts):

✅ PART 3 · AVANT D'ENVOYER 12 min

Record once, listen back, and check yourself against this list. You may re-record before you submit — use that buffer.

□ I said 6–8 sentences and introduced my family.
□ Every adjective agrees (feminine ending when it describes a woman).
□ I used at least one irregular adjective (beau / nouveau / vieux).
□ My BANGS adjective is placed before its noun.
□ I used the verb faire in a full sentence.
□ I can hear my own words clearly on playback.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +8 min
Record-and-compare: play paragraph 1 of Voici ma famille from Lesson 1.05, then record yourself saying the same lines. Listen back-to-back and note one sound to fix. Imitation of a native model is the fastest route to a French accent.
