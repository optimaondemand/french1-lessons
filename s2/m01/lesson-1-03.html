Lesson 1.03
⚜ La règle BANGS — Where the Adjective Goes
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
In English the adjective comes first: a kind sister. In French, usually it comes after — but not always.
Yesterday you learned how adjectives change. Today you learn where they sit. Most French adjectives follow the noun (une famille sympathique). But a small group — captured by the word BANGS — jumps in front. Learn the five categories and you'll place adjectives like a native.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to explain the BANGS rule and name the five categories that go before the noun.
2
I will be able to place an adjective correctly, before or after the noun, without help.
📌 PART 1 · LA RÈGLE PAR DÉFAUT — APRÈS LE NOM 9 min

Most adjectives follow the noun

Default rule. In French, the great majority of adjectives come after the noun: une famille sympathique, un frère sérieux, une sœur curieuse. Almost all of this module's character words work this way.
  • C’est une famille sympathique.adjective AFTER the noun (the normal case)
🧠 PART 2 · LES EXCEPTIONS — B.A.N.G.S. 14 min

Five kinds that jump in front

The BANGS rule. Adjectives of Beauty, Age, Number, Goodness, and Size come before the noun. Most of yesterday's irregulars (beau, nouveau, vieux) live here, and so do the everyday words below.
LetterCategoryExamplesIn a phrase
BBeautybeau, joliun beau garçon
AAgejeune, vieux, nouveauun vieux chien
NNumberpremier, deux, troisle premier enfant
GGoodnessbon, mauvais, gentilun bon frère
SSizegrand, petit, grosune grande famille

Listen — this sentence packs two BANGS adjectives, both in front of their nouns:

  • J’ai un bon frère et une grande famille.bon (Goodness) + grande (Size), both BEFORE the noun
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Say the mnemonic out loud until it sticks: B-A-N-G-S — Beauty, Age, Number, Goodness, Size. Then write one phrase for each letter, using a family member: ma jolie cousine, mon vieux grand-père, mon premier neveu…
📋 PART 3 · LE TABLEAU DE PLACEMENT 9 min

Before or after — side by side

Read each row aloud. Notice the pattern: BANGS in front, everything else behind.

✍️ PART 4 · À TON TOUR 8 min

Put the adjective in the right place. Commit to an answer first — write each phrase out — then reveal to check. Trying before you see the answer is what makes it stick.

🧠 PLACE THE ADJECTIVE
Build these four phrases: (1) a good father · (2) a friendly family · (3) an old grandmother · (4) a serious sister.
a good fatherun bon père — bon = Goodness, BEFORE
a friendly familyune famille sympathique — personality, AFTER
an old grandmotherune vieille grand-mère — vieux = Age, BEFORE + feminine
a serious sisterune sœur sérieuse — character, AFTER + feminine
If you placed all four correctly, you've got BANGS cold. If not, re-read the mnemonic and try Part 3 once more.
