La règle BANGS

Where the Adjective Goes · about 50 minutes
Le tableau du module : Pierre-Auguste Renoir, Le Déjeuner des canotiers (Luncheon of the Boating Party), 1880–81
TODAY'S FOCUS
In English the adjective comes first: a kind sister. In French, usually it comes after — but not always.
Yesterday you learned how adjectives change. Today you learn where they sit. Most French adjectives follow the noun (une famille sympathique). But a small group — captured by the word BANGS — jumps in front. Learn the five categories and you'll place adjectives like a native. Then two short practice cards bring back this module’s words: first by ear, then typed with their accents.

ILA RÈGLE PAR DÉFAUT — APRÈS LE NOM7 min

Most adjectives follow the noun

Default rule. In French, the great majority of adjectives come after the noun: une famille sympathique, un frère sérieux, une sœur curieuse. Almost all of this module's character words work this way.
  • C’est une famille sympathique.adjective AFTER the noun (the normal case)
IILES EXCEPTIONS — B.A.N.G.S.14 min

Five kinds that jump in front

The BANGS rule. Adjectives of Beauty, Age, Number, Goodness, and Size come before the noun. Most of yesterday's irregulars (beau, nouveau, vieux) live here, and so do the everyday words below.
LetterCategoryExamplesIn a phrase
BBeautybeau (handsome), joli (pretty)un beau garçon
AAgejeune (young), vieux (old), nouveau (new)un vieux chien
NNumberpremier (first), deux (two), trois (three)le premier enfant (the first child)
GGoodnessbon (good), mauvais (bad), gentil (kind)un bon frère
SSizegrand (big / tall), petit (small / little), gros (big / fat)une grande famille

Listen — this sentence packs two BANGS adjectives, both in front of their nouns:

  • J’ai un bon frère et une grande famille.bon (Goodness) + grande (Size), both BEFORE the noun
Dans le tableau · In the paintingBANGS chez Renoir

Remember Renoir’s Le Déjeuner des canotiers from the start of the module? Look at three of its people. Each adjective is a BANGS adjective, so it sits in front of the noun.

Find someone in the painting who looks sympathique (friendly). Where does sympathique go: before or after the noun? (click to check)
After: une fille sympathique, un homme sympathique. Personality is not BANGS, so it follows the noun.
IIILE TABLEAU DE PLACEMENT4 min

Before or after — side by side

A quick reference before you practise: BANGS in front, everything else behind.

Adjective typeGoes…Example
