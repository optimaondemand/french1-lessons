⚜ Set B & Irregular Adjectives — beau, nouveau, vieux

📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday's words followed the rules. Today you meet three that don't — the most useful, most stubborn adjectives in French.
First, ten more words — more character traits, plus beau, nouveau, vieux and the family words for the eldest and youngest child. Then your first grammar rule: these three adjectives change shape not once but three ways, and knowing them is a mark of real French.

🧠 PART 2 · LA GRAMMAIRE — TROIS ADJECTIFS IRRÉGULIERS 17 min

Three forms, not two

Most French adjectives have two forms — masculine and feminine (grand / grande). But beau, nouveau, and vieux have a third: a special masculine form used before a vowel sound, so the words glide together. Learn all three columns.

MeaningMasculineBefore a vowel (m.)Feminine
beautiful / handsomebeau (un beau garçon)bel (un bel homme)belle (une belle fille)
newnouveau (un nouveau vélo)nouvel (un nouvel ami)nouvelle (une nouvelle robe)
oldvieux (un vieux chien)vieil (un vieil arbre)vieille (une vieille maison)

Hear the feminine and the before-a-vowel forms in real sentences. Notice how nouvel glides straight into appartement:

  • Ma sœur est belle et gentille.beau → belle (feminine)
  • Nous avons un nouvel appartement.nouveau → nouvel (before a vowel)
  • C’est une vieille maison.vieux → vieille (feminine)
✅ PART 3 · VÉRIFIE TA COMPRÉHENSION 10 min

Pick the correct form for each noun. You get instant feedback — this is practice, not a grade.

✎ ITEM 3
