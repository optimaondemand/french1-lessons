🎤 PART 1 · PHONÉTIQUE 13 min

The j'ai glide and the -é ending

The glide. j'ai is one smooth sound — “zhay” — and it links straight into the participle: j'ai⁠_mangé, j'ai⁠_fait. Don't put a pause between them; the whole past verb rides in one breath.
  • j'ai mangéglide + -é ending
  • j'ai faitglide + irregular (silent t)
The -é trap. The participle -é sounds exactly like the infinitive -er (both “ay”). What tells a listener it's the past is the little avoir word in front — a, as, ont. Say the avoir clearly and the past becomes unmistakable.
  • il a jouéhear the a before joué
  • je n'ai pas regardénegative: n'ai pas around avoir

Shadow it: play each clip, pause, and copy the exact rhythm three times. Aim to match the glide, not just the words.

🎤 Speaking Task · Mon week-end dernier
15 pts · 1 submission
SUBMITTED IN CANVAS · MEDIA RECORDING 15 min
  • Record 6–8 sentences describing what you did last weekend, in order — all in the passé composé.
  • Use at least five verbs in the past, one irregular participle (fait, vu, pris), one negative sentence (je n'ai pas…), and one time expression (hier soir, samedi).
  • Speak from notes, not a script read word-for-word — natural delivery counts.
  • This is your Day 4 / Day 6 writing, spoken aloud. You've already built it.

How it's scored (15 pts):

Pronunciation & Fluency5Clear j'ai glide, smooth links, steady pace

✅ PART 3 · AVANT D'ENVOYER 12 min

Record once, listen back, and check yourself against this list. You may re-record before you submit — use that buffer.

□ I said 6–8 sentences, all in the past.
□ Every past verb has avoir + a participle.
□ My participle endings are right (-é / -i / fait, vu, pris).
□ My negative sentence wraps avoir (je n'ai pas…).
□ I used an irregular participle and a time expression.
□ I can hear my own words clearly on playback.
