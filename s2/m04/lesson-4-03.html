Lesson 4.03
⚜ Les participes — -ir Verbs & the Irregulars
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday's -é was the easy case. Today you learn the two others: -ir verbs and the irregulars you can't avoid.
The formula never changes — still avoir + participle. Only the participle changes shape. Regular -ir verbs take -i (finir → fini). And three of the most useful French verbs are irregular: faire → fait, voir → vu, prendre → pris. You just have to know them.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to form the participle of a regular -ir verb (-i) and recall the irregulars fait, vu, pris.
2
I will be able to conjugate a verb in the passé composé through all six persons without help.
🎵 PART 1 · LES VERBES EN -IR 10 min

Regular -ir verbs take -i

Rule. A regular -ir verb drops -ir and adds -i: finir → fini, choisir → choisi. The helping verb is still avoir: nous avons fini = we finished.
  • Nous avons fini nos devoirs.We finished our homework.
⭐ PART 2 · LES PARTICIPES IRRÉGULIERS 10 min

Three you must simply memorize

Some verbs make a participle that follows no rule. These three are the ones you'll use constantly — learn them as fixed words:

InfinitiveParticipleExample
faire (to do/make)faitj'ai fait du sport
voir (to see)vuj'ai vu un film
prendre (to take)prisj'ai pris le bus
  • J'ai fait du sport ce week-end.I did sports this weekend.
📋 PART 3 · LE TABLEAU COMPLET — JOUER 12 min

All six persons, one verb

This is the model for every verb in the passé composé: only the avoir part moves; the participle stays fixed. Read each row aloud.

PersonForm (jouer → joué)Meaning

tutu as jouéyou played
il / elle / onil a jouéhe / she / one played
nousnous avons jouéwe played
vousvous avez jouéyou (formal/plural) played

✍️ PART 4 · À TON TOUR 8 min

Now conjugate faire (participle fait) in the passé composé, through all six persons — on paper or in your head. Commit to an answer first, then reveal to check. Trying before you see the answer is what makes it stick.

🧠 CONJUGATE · faire (passé composé)
Write all six forms of faire in the past, then reveal.
j'ai faitnous avons fait

The participle fait never changes — only avoir moves. If you matched all six, you've got the whole tense.
