⚜ Évaluations — Vocabulary Check + Langue en action
Assessment day · about 50 minutes
Today you take two graded assessments (45 pts total). Both are completed in Canvas, not on this page — this page is your briefing and your launch point. Read each card, then click through. Between the two cards there is a short, ungraded typed-forms warm-up for Langue en action.
⏰ AVANT DE COMMENCER 5 min
💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
How ready do you feel to be tested by ear?
1 · Vocabulary Check
25 pts · 1 attempt
COMPLETED IN CANVAS · AUTO-GRADED · should be submitted by the end of the module
  • Listening-based. About three-quarters of the items are ear-only: hear a word, choose or type the meaning; some are dictation (write what you hear).
  • One attempt, no retakes — so warm up your ear first (see the check-in above).
  • Scope: all 20 Module 4 words. Headphones recommended; you can replay each clip.
  • Auto-graded — you'll see your score immediately.
▶ To take the Vocabulary Check: return to Module 4 in Canvas and open the Vocabulary Check — it is in the module’s item list, below the eight lessons.
