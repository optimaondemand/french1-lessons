Lesson 4.01
⚜ Le temps libre — Talking About the Past
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Everything so far has happened now. This week you learn to say what you did — and it changes everything.
This module is about free time — films, music, sport, parties — and about the single most important new tool of the semester: the past tense. Today you meet the words for leisure and for when something happened. You will hear each one spoken before you ever try to say it.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce ten leisure and past-time expressions.
2
I will be able to match each spoken word to its meaning by ear.
📋 WHAT YOU NEED FOR THIS LESSON
▶
Headphones or speakers — today is a listening day.
▶
Your vocabulary notebook or flashcard app.
⏰ PART 1 · SE METTRE EN ROUTE 12 min

First, a quick look back

Up to now, every French sentence you have built lives in the present: je joue au foot (I play soccer), je regarde un film (I watch a movie). But real conversation is full of the past — what you did last night, last weekend, last week. That is what this module unlocks. To get there, you first need the words for free-time activities and for pointing at the past.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
What is your favorite way to spend your free time?
📚 PART 2 · LE VOCABULAIRE DU TEMPS LIBRE 18 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 on each word and say it back out loud. The first group names when the past happened (hier, le week-end dernier…) — the words that will always sit next to your new past tense. The rest are the free-time activities you'll talk about doing.

  • le passéthe past
  • hieryesterday
  • hier soirlast night
  • le week-end dernierlast weekend
  • la semaine dernièrelast week
  • venirto come
  • regarder un filmto watch a movie
  • écouter de la musiqueto listen to music
  • jouer aux jeux vidéoto play video games
  • faire du sportto do / play sports
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Go down the list a second time, but this time shadow each word: play it, pause, and repeat it out loud three times before moving on. Shadowing is the single fastest way to make new sounds feel natural.
📝 PART 3 · RÉVISE TON VOCABULAIRE 10 min

Drill the words with the Vocabulaire page

You don’t have to build your word bank from scratch. Open the Module 4 Vocabulaire study page (in this module) — every word, its meaning, and its audio in one place, as flip cards you can drill by ear. Because your Vocabulary Check is listening-based, practice by hearing the word and recalling its meaning.

Making your own paper or digital cards on top of that is a great memory booster — but it’s optional, not required.
