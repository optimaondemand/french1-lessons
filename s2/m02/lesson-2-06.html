  • Listening-based. 15 of the 25 items are by ear: 8 times you hear a word and choose its meaning, and 7 times you type what you hear (in French, with the article and accents). The other 10 are written: match French and English words, give an opposite, and say which region is where.

N’oublie pas ! · Don’t forget!

Langue en action should be submitted by the end of Module 2 (Lesson 2.08). It is worth 20 points. If you don’t finish it today, come back to it before you close the module.

  • Structured production. Three parts: choose the right demonstrative (ce/cet/cette/ces), supply the right preposition of place (en/au/dans le…de), and short comprehension from Un tour des régions.
  • Auto-graded (fill-in-the-blank, multiple-choice, passage items).
  • Review your Day 4 sentences and your Day 5 reading, Un tour des régions: the passage questions come from that tour.
