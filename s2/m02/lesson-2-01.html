⚜ Les paysages — Landscapes & Regions
Lesson · about 50 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
France is far more than Paris. How does the land — sea, mountain, coast, countryside — shape who its people are?
This module is about les régions. Today you meet ten words for France's famous regions and its landscapes — the sea, the mountains, the beach, the forest. You will hear each one spoken before you try it, because a place you can hear is a place you can picture. To finish, you'll open the Carte de France and start placing every region on the map.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce four famous French regions and six landscape words.
2
I will be able to match each spoken place-word to its meaning by ear.
3
I will be able to find the four module regions, and then the rest of the 13 metropolitan regions, on the interactive map.
📋 WHAT YOU NEED FOR THIS LESSON
▶
Headphones or speakers — today is a listening day.
▶
Your vocabulary notebook or flashcard app.
⏰ PART 1 · SE METTRE EN ROUTE 12 min

First, a quick look back

In Module 1 you learned irregular adjectives to describe people — beau, vieux, nouveau. This module turns those same describing skills onto places: a region can be belle, a village joli, a coast magnifique. Keep those adjectives warm — you'll use them all week.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
If you could visit one kind of place in France, which pulls you most?
📚 PART 2 · LE VOCABULAIRE DES RÉGIONS 18 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 on each word and say it back out loud. The first four are famous regions; the next six are the landscapes you find across France. Notice most region names are feminine (la…) — that will matter on Day 3.

  • la régionthe region
  • la BretagneBrittany
  • l’AlsaceAlsace
  • la ProvenceProvence
  • la NormandieNormandy
  • la montagnethe mountain
  • la merthe sea
  • la plagethe beach
  • la campagnethe countryside
  • la forêtthe forest
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Go down the list a second time, but this time shadow each word: play it, pause, and repeat it out loud three times before moving on. Shadowing is the single fastest way to make new sounds feel natural.
📝 PART 3 · RÉVISE TON VOCABULAIRE 10 min

Drill the words with the Vocabulaire page

You don’t have to build your word bank from scratch. Open the Module 2 Vocabulaire study page (in this module) — every word, its meaning, and its audio in one place, as flip cards you can drill by ear. Because your Vocabulary Check is listening-based, practice by hearing the word and recalling its meaning.

Making your own paper or digital cards on top of that is a great memory booster — but it’s optional, not required.

🗺️ La Carte de France
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
EXPLORER + QUIZ MODES 18 min
  • Explorer mode: hover and click to place the 13 metropolitan regions — find your four module regions first (Bretagne to the west, Alsace to the east, Provence to the south, Normandie to the north), then learn the rest.
  • Quiz mode: timed click-to-find. Say each region's name aloud as you click. By Day 3 you'll be able to add its compass direction too — « La Bretagne est à l’ouest. » Your score, errors, and time are shown; restart as often as you like.
  • This is where you build the geography for the whole course. Mastering all 13 feeds the once-per-semester Carte extra-credit challenge in the capstone module.
