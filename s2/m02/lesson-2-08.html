Grammar Quiz · Démonstratifs & prépositions
30 pts · 2 attempts
COMPLETED IN CANVAS · AUTO-GRADED · should be submitted by the end of the module 30 min
  • Listening items (~15 pts): hear a sentence, then answer — identify the demonstrative, the preposition, the region, or the meaning. Headphones recommended; you can replay each clip.
  • Grammar items (~15 pts): choose or supply the correct demonstrative (ce/cet/cette/ces) and preposition of place (en/au/dans le…de).
  • Two attempts — your higher score counts. Review any misses before your second try.
▶ To take the quiz: return to Module 2 in Canvas and open the Grammar Quiz — it is in the module’s item list, below the eight lessons.
IIRÉVISE TES ERREURS5 min

After your first attempt, look at what you missed. Most Module 2 errors fall into three buckets: wrong demonstrative for the noun's gender/number, en vs au confusion, or a misplaced compass phrase (dans le sud de vs à l’ouest de). Name which one caught you, fix that one thing, and take your second attempt.

IIIAU REVOIR, MONET 5 minpractice · not graded

CLÔTURE DU MODULE 2 — CE QUE TU SAIS FAIRE
  • I can name and understand 20 region & landscape words by ear.
  • I can use demonstrative adjectives (ce, cet, cette, ces) to match a noun.
  • I can use geographical prepositions — en Bretagne, au Canada, dans le sud de la France.
  • I can describe a region: where it is, its landscape, and what it's like.
  • I can read and understand a short tour of France and its regional identities.
