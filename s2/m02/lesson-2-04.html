Lesson 2.04
⚜ Mise en pratique — Describe a Region
Lesson · about 50 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You know the words and the rules. Today you use them — choosing, writing, and (for the first time) writing down what you hear.
Three moves, each harder than the last: pick the right demonstrative or preposition, write your own description of a region, then listen to a dictation and write down what you hear. Dictation is the bridge between hearing French and producing it — exactly what your listening-based Vocabulary Check rewards. A short practice round on ce / cet / cette / ces and the prepositions of place closes the day.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to choose the correct demonstrative / preposition in a full sentence and write my own region description.
2
I will be able to understand a spoken sentence about geography well enough to write it down.
3
I will be able to choose the right demonstrative and preposition of place across seven mixed items.
✅ PART 1 · CHOISIS LA BONNE FORME 12 min

Four sentences. Pick the form that fits. Instant feedback — practice, not a grade.

✎ ITEM 1
______ côte est magnifique.  (côte = feminine)
✎ ITEM 2
Cet été, nous allons ______ Bretagne.  (Bretagne = feminine region)
✎ ITEM 3
La Provence se trouve ______ sud de la France.  (le sud)
✎ ITEM 4
______ villages sont célèbres en Alsace.  (villages = plural)
✍️ PART 2 · À TOI D'ÉCRIRE 13 min

Write three sentences describing a French region

In your notebook, choose one region (Bretagne, Alsace, Provence, or Normandie) and write three sentences: where it is (compass), what landscape it has, and what it's like (an adjective from Module 1). Use at least one demonstrative (cette région…).

Model: Cette région se trouve dans le sud de la France. Il y a la mer, des plages et un climat ensoleillé. La Provence est très belle !

Keep these — you'll reuse them for your Speaking Task on Day 7. Check each sentence against the three rules before you move on.

🎧 PART 3 · LA DICTÉE 15 min

Hear it, type it, check it

Play each clip (as many times as you need). Type exactly what you hear — accents and all — then press Check. Use Reveal only after you have tried. Don't reveal early; the struggle is the learning.

Accents: — click a box first, then a letter.
1Play, type, then check.
Ce village est très joli.
2Listen for the feminine form.
Cette région est au sud.
3A plural — catch the ces.
Ces montagnes sont magnifiques.
4Listen for the preposition en.
Cet été, je vais en Bretagne.
5A locating sentence — catch dans le sud de.
Cette région se trouve dans le sud de la France.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +10 min
Spiral to Module 1 (irregular adjectives): write four sentences that each describe a region and use an irregular adjective — cette belle côte, ce vieux village, ces nouveaux villages… Pairing demonstrative + irregular adjective is exactly what the Speaking Task rewards.
✏️ Accord & conjugaison — entraînement
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
DEMONSTRATIVES + PLACE 12 min
Seven items on Module 2’s grammar: choosing ce / cet / cette / ces, and saying where a place is.
✎ ITEM 1
______ région est magnifique. (f.)
✎ ITEM 2
______ village est très joli. (m.)
✎ ITEM 3
______ endroit est vieux. (m., starts with a vowel)
✎ ITEM 4
______ montagnes sont magnifiques. (pl.)
✎ ITEM 5
La Bretagne se trouve ______ l’ouest de la France.
✎ ITEM 6
Cet été, je vais ______ Provence.
✎ ITEM 7
Il y a un beau village ______ la côte.
