📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Sharpen the sound, then speak — a region of your choice, out loud, on the record.
First a quick clinic on two very French sounds hiding in this module's map: the nasal vowel in en and the soft “gn” in Bretagne and montagne. Then you'll record your Speaking Task — the region description you've been building all module. You'll close with a from-memory run of the preposition table and a quick note on tomorrow's Grammar Quiz.
🎤 PART 1 · PHONÉTIQUE 13 min

Two sounds on the French map

The nasal vowel en / an — heard in en, Normandie, grande. Send the air through your nose; do not pronounce the n as a separate letter. It's one smooth “ahn” sound, not “enn.”
  • … je vais en Bretagnenasal en + the gn of Bretagne
  • … de belles plages en Normandienasal en + Normandie
The soft “gn” [ɲ] — in Bretagne, montagne, campagne. It's the sound in English “canyon” or Spanish mañana — a single melted ny, never a hard “g.”
  • Ces montagnes sont magnifiques.the gn of montagne

Shadow it: play each clip, pause, and copy the exact sound three times. Aim to match the nasal glide and the melted gn, not just the words.

🎤 Speaking Task · Décris une région
15 pts · 1 submission
SUBMITTED IN CANVAS · MEDIA RECORDING · should be submitted by the end of the module 15 min
  • Record 6–8 sentences describing one French region of your choice (Bretagne, Alsace, Provence, or Normandie).
  • Say where it is (a compass phrase — dans le sud de…), name at least three landscape words, use at least one demonstrative (cette région, ces plages) and the preposition en.
  • You are encouraged to prepare a script to help you get ready for your recording.
  • This is your Day 4 / Day 5 writing, spoken aloud. You've already built it.

How it's scored (15 pts):

CriterionPtsWhat earns full marks
Task Completion56–8 sentences with the required elements (location, landscapes, demonstrative)
Language Accuracy5Correct demonstratives & prepositions; gender agreement handled
Pronunciation & Fluency5Clear nasal vowels, a soft gn, steady pace
▶ To record your Speaking Task: return to Module 2 in Canvas and open the Speaking Task — it is in the module’s item list, below the eight lessons.
✅ PART 2 · AVANT D'ENVOYER 12 min

Record once, listen back, and check yourself against this list. You may re-record before you submit — use that buffer.

□ I said 6–8 sentences.
□ Every demonstrative matches its noun (cette région, ce village, ces plages).
□ I used en for “to / in” the region.
□ I gave a compass location (dans le sud de…, à l’ouest de…).
□ My nasal vowels and the gn sound are smooth.
□ I can hear my own words clearly on playback.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +8 min
Record-and-compare: play paragraph 2 of Un tour des régions from Lesson 2.05, then record yourself saying the same lines. Listen back-to-back and note one sound to fix. Imitating a native model is the fastest route to a French accent.
📋 PART 3 · LE TABLEAU, ENCORE UNE FOIS 13 min

From memory, fill in the right preposition for each place. Commit first — then reveal.

🧠 COMPLETE · en / au / dans le…de / à l’…de
  1. Je vais ______ Normandie.
  2. Ils habitent ______ Japon.
  3. Nice se trouve ______ sud de la France.
  4. Strasbourg est ______ est de la France.
1. Je vais en Normandie.feminine region → en
2. … habitent au Japon.masculine country → au
3. … se trouve dans le sud de la France.le sud → dans le sud de
4. … est à l’est de la France.l’est → à l’est de
If all four matched, you're ready for the Grammar Quiz. If not, run the Day 3 table twice more before tomorrow's quiz.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +10 min · cumulative spiral
Mix Module 2 with earlier work: write four sentences that each combine a region + an irregular adjective from Module 1 + a demonstrative — ce vieux village, cette belle côte, ce nouveau quartier. Interleaving old and new is what makes review actually stick.

Tomorrow is the Grammar Quiz — the last graded piece of Module 2, and it includes listening items. Tonight, run the Day 3 preposition table once and listen to any three example sentences on the earlier lessons. That's all the prep you need.
