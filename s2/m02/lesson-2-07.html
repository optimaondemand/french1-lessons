Lesson 2.07 · Jour d'évaluation
⚜ Évaluations — Vocabulary Check + Langue en action
Assessment day · about 40 minutes
Two graded assessments open today (45 pts total). Both are completed in Canvas, not on this page — this page is your briefing and your launch point. Read each card, then click through.
⏰ AVANT DE COMMENCER 5 min
💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
How ready do you feel to be tested by ear?
1 · Vocabulary Check
25 pts · 1 attempt
  • Listening-based. About three-quarters of the items are ear-only: hear a place-word, choose or type the meaning; some are dictation (write what you hear).
  • One attempt, no retakes — so warm up your ear first (see the check-in above).
  • Scope: all 20 Module 2 words — regions, landscapes, coast, climate, and the compass. Headphones recommended; you can replay each clip.
  • Auto-graded — you'll see your score immediately.
▶ To take the Vocabulary Check: return to Module 2 in Canvas and open the Vocabulary Check — it is in the module’s item list, below the ten leçons.
2 · Langue en action
20 pts
  • Structured production. Three parts: choose the right demonstrative (ce/cet/cette/ces), supply the right preposition of place (en/au/dans le…de), and short comprehension from Un tour des régions.
  • Auto-graded (fill-in-the-blank, multiple-choice, passage items).
  • Lean on your Day 4 and Day 6 sentences — you've already written most of what this asks.
▶ To complete Langue en action: return to Module 2 in Canvas and open the Langue en action — it is in the module’s item list, below the ten leçons.
