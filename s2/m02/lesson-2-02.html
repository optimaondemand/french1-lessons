IILA GRAMMAIRE — LES ADJECTIFS DÉMONSTRATIFS12 min

How French says “this” and “these”

A demonstrative adjective points at a specific thing: this region, these mountains. English has just two words (this / these); French has four, and the form must match the noun's gender and number. That's Rule 1.

FormUse it before…Example
cea masculine singular noun (consonant)ce village
ceta masculine singular noun before a vowel or mute hcet endroit, cet été (summer)
cettea feminine singular nouncette région
cesany plural noun (masc. or fem.)ces montagnes

Hear all four in a real sentence. Notice the form changes to fit the noun that follows:

  • Ce village est très joli.This village is very pretty. (ce + masc.)
  • Cet endroit est célèbre.This place is famous. (cet before a vowel)
  • Cette région est au sud.This region is in the south. (cette + fem.)
  • Ces montagnes sont magnifiques.These mountains are magnificent. (ces + plural)
