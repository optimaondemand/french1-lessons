Lesson 2.03
⚜ Aller & se situer — Geographical Prepositions
Lesson · about 50 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You can point at a region. Today you learn to travel to it — and to say exactly where on the map it sits.
Two small rules do most of the work: which little word means “to / in” a place (en Bretagne vs au Canada), and how to locate a region with the compass (dans le sud de la France). Then you'll build the full reference table — and try one yourself before you see the answer. Two short practice cards close the day: a word recall and a build-it-yourself challenge that uses all three rules.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to use en with feminine regions/countries and au with masculine countries.
2
I will be able to locate a region with the compass (dans le sud de…, à l'ouest de…).
3
I will be able to recall this module's words and build short sentences that point at, travel to, and locate a region.
✈️ PART 1 · RÈGLE N°2 — « TO / IN » A PLACE 11 min

Going to a region or country

Rule 2. Use en before a feminine region or country (most end in -e): en Bretagne, en Provence, en Normandie, en France. Use au before a masculine country: au Canada, au Japon. (Plural countries take aux: aux États-Unis.)

Almost every French region is feminine, so en is your everyday word for “to” a region. Listen:

  • Cet été, je vais en Bretagne.This summer, I'm going to Brittany.
  • Il y a de belles plages en Normandie.There are beautiful beaches in Normandy.
🧭 PART 2 · RÈGLE N°3 — SE SITUER SUR LA CARTE 11 min

Saying where a region is

Rule 3. To locate a place, use dans le + compass point + de: dans le sud de la France (in the south of France). Or use à l’ / au + compass point + de: à l’ouest de Paris (to the west of Paris), au nord de Lyon.

The four directions from Day 2 do the work: le nord, le sud, l’est, l’ouest. Listen to a full locating sentence:

  • Cette région se trouve dans le sud de la France.This region is located in the south of France.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Spiral back to Module 1: pair a locating phrase with an irregular adjective you already know. Cette belle région se trouve à l’ouest — adjective + preposition together. Write two of your own.
📋 PART 3 · LE TABLEAU DE RÉFÉRENCE 12 min

Every preposition, at a glance

This is your map toolkit for the whole module. Read each row aloud.

PrepositionUse with…Example

✍️ PART 4 · À TON TOUR 8 min

Fill each blank with the right preposition — on paper or in your head. Commit to an answer first, then reveal to check. Trying before you see the answer is what makes it stick.

🧠 COMPLETE · en / au / dans le…de
Fill the four blanks, then reveal.
  1. Je vais ______ Provence cet été.
  2. Mon cousin habite ______ Canada.
  3. La Bretagne se trouve ______ ouest de la France.
  4. Il y a des montagnes ______ sud de la France.
1. Je vais en Provence.Provence is feminine → en
2. … habite au Canada.Canada is a masculine country → au
3. … se trouve à l’ouest de la France.l’ouest → à l’ouest de
4. … des montagnes dans le sud de la France.le sud → dans le sud de
If you matched all four, you've got the geographical prepositions cold — the heart of this module.

PRACTICE · UNGRADED
BUILD IT YOURSELF 5 min
Build each one in your head — or out loud — before you reveal the answer.
⚡ DÉFI 1
Say it in French: This region is in the south.
Cette région est dans le sud.
région is feminine, so cette.
⚡ DÉFI 2
Say it in French: These villages are very pretty.
Ces villages sont très jolis.
Plural demonstrative ces, and the adjective agrees: jolis.
⚡ DÉFI 3
Where is Alsace? Answer in French.
L’Alsace est à l’est de la France.
à l’est — direction takes à.
