Prendre & le partitif

Ordering & “Some” · about 55 minutes
Le tableau du module : Paul Cézanne, Pommes et oranges (Still Life with Apples and Oranges), c. 1899
TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you learned the parts of a meal. Today you learn the one verb that lets you order them — and how to say “some.”
First, ten more words — quantities and the table. Then the workhorse verb of the French table: prendre (to have / to take). At a restaurant you don't “eat” a dish, you take (prendre) it. We'll also review the partitive (du, de la, de l', des) — how French says “some” cheese, “some” salad. You'll finish with a short ear-quiz — hear a word, choose its meaning — the exact skill your Vocabulary Check rewards.

IILE VERBE PRENDRE12 min

The verb you order with

Prendre means “to take,” but at the table it means “to have” a dish or drink: Je prends une salade. (I'll have a salad.) It's irregular — the singular forms are easy, but watch the double n in ils prennent.

SubjectForm of prendreExample
jeprendsje prends une entrée
tuprendstu prends un dessert
il / elle / onprendelle prend de la soupe
nousprenonsnous prenons le menu
vousprenezvous prenez une boisson
ils / ellesprennentils prennent le plat du jour (the dish of the day)

Hear three of them in real restaurant sentences:

  • Je prends une entrée et un plat principal.I'll have a starter and a main course.
  • Elle prend de la soupe.She's having some soup.
  • Nous prenons le menu à vingt euros.We'll take the twenty-euro set menu.
IIILE PARTITIF — SAYING “SOME”13 min

When you have some of a food (not all of it, not a specific one), French uses the partitive. It matches the gender and number of the noun:

ArticleUsed before…Example
dumasculine noundu fromage — some cheese
de lafeminine nounde la salade — some salad
de l'before a vowelde l'eau — some water
desplural noundes légumes — some vegetables
  • Je voudrais du fromage et de la salade.I would like some cheese and some salad.

Choose the partitive that fits. Instant feedback — this is practice, not a grade.

✎ ITEM 1
Elle prend ______ soupe.  (la soupe is feminine)
✎ ITEM 2
Je voudrais ______ fromage.  (le fromage is masculine)
✎ ITEM 3
Nous prenons ______ légumes.  (les légumes is plural)
✎ ITEM 4
Je voudrais ______ eau.  (l’eau starts with a vowel)

Items 5–8: no hint this time. Think of the word’s article (le, la, les): it tells you which partitive to use.
