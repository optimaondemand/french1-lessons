🎤 PART 1 · PHONÉTIQUE — LA VOYELLE NASALE 13 min

The nasal in prends

In the singular, je prends and elle prend carry a nasal vowel — the “an” sound (like in France). Let the air go through your nose; do not pronounce the final d or s. It sounds like “prahn.”
  • Je prends…nasal “an” — no final s
  • Elle prend…nasal “an” — no final d
In the plural, the sound opens up: nous prenons is not nasal on the stem — you clearly hear “pruh-NOHN.” The nasal moves to the ending -ons. Hear the difference:
  • Nous prenons…clear “pruh” + nasal ending

Shadow it: play each clip, pause, and copy the exact sound three times. Aim to nasalize prends and open up prenons — that contrast is what marks a French ear.

🎤 Speaking Task · Au restaurant
15 pts · 1 submission
SUBMITTED IN CANVAS · MEDIA RECORDING 15 min
  • Record 6–8 sentences ordering a full meal at a restaurant, in order.
  • Use prendre at least twice, at least one partitive (du / de la / des), and at least one quantity expression (une tranche de, une bouteille de…). Open with Je voudrais commander and close with L'addition, s'il vous plaît.
  • Speak from notes, not a script read word-for-word — natural delivery counts.
  • This is your Day 4 / Day 6 order, spoken aloud. You've already built it.

How it's scored (15 pts):

CriterionPtsWhat earns full marks
Task Completion56–8 sentences, in order, with the required elements
Language Accuracy5Correct prendre forms; partitive and quantity de handled
Pronunciation & Fluency5Nasal prends, smooth polite phrases, steady pace

✅ PART 3 · AVANT D'ENVOYER 12 min

Record once, listen back, and check yourself against this list. You may re-record before you submit — use that buffer.

□ I said 6–8 sentences.
□ I used prendre correctly at least twice (right form for the subject).
□ My partitive matches the noun (du fromage, de la salade, des légumes).
□ My quantity expression uses plain de (une bouteille d'eau).
□ I opened politely and closed with l'addition.
□ My prends is nasal and I can hear my words clearly on playback.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +8 min
Record-and-compare: play paragraph 2 of the reading from Lesson 3.05, then record yourself saying the same lines. Listen back-to-back and note one sound to fix. Imitation of a native model is the fastest route to a French accent.
