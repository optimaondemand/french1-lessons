Grammar Quiz · Prendre, le partitif & les quantités
30 pts · 2 attempts
COMPLETED IN CANVAS · AUTO-GRADED 30 min
  • Listening items (~15 pts): hear a sentence, then answer — identify the verb form, the partitive, or the meaning. Headphones recommended; you can replay each clip.
  • Grammar items (~15 pts): conjugate prendre, choose the right partitive (du / de la / de l' / des), and apply the quantity rule (plain de).
  • Two attempts — your higher score counts. Review any misses before your second try.
▶ Open the Grammar Quiz in Canvas
🔎 PART 3 · RÉVISE TES ERREURS 5 min

After your first attempt, look at what you missed. Most Module 3 errors fall into three buckets: a wrong prendre form (watch nous prenons / ils prennent), the wrong partitive for the noun's gender, or using a partitive where a quantity word forces plain de. Name which one caught you, fix that one thing, and take your second attempt.

🏁 CLÔTURE DU MODULE 3 — CE QUE TU SAIS FAIRE
  • I can name and understand 20 restaurant and food words by ear.
  • I can conjugate prendre through all six persons.
  • I can choose the right partitive (du / de la / de l' / des) and use the quantity de.
  • I can order a full meal in the correct sequence, in writing and out loud.
  • I can read and understand a full French passage about a meal at a restaurant.
