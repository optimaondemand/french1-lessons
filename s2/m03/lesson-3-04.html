Lesson 3.04
⚜ Commander au restaurant — Order a Full Meal
Lesson · about 50 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You have the words, the verb, and the quantities. Today you run the whole scene — from sitting down to asking for the bill.
The ordering sequence is a fixed little dance: greet, ask for the menu, order with Je voudrais… or Je prends…, and close with l'addition, s'il vous plaît. Then you'll write your own order and listen to a dictation and write down what you hear. You'll close with two short practice rounds on prendre, the partitive and the polite order.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to order a full meal in the correct sequence, from greeting to l'addition.
2
I will be able to understand a spoken restaurant sentence well enough to write it down.
3
I will be able to choose the right form of prendre and the right partitive in a sentence.
🍽️ PART 1 · LA SÉQUENCE POUR COMMANDER 10 min

The order of ordering

The polite formula. Je voudrais… (“I would like…”) is softer than Je veux (“I want”) — always use je voudrais or je prends with the waiter. Close with L'addition, s'il vous plaît.
Ordering for the table. When you order for more than yourself, je voudrais becomes nous voudrions (“we would like”): Nous voudrions deux cafés, s'il vous plaît. (You'll meet the whole verb in Module 9 — for now, just these two.)

Listen to the pieces of a real order:

  • Je voudrais commander, s'il vous plaît.I would like to order, please.
  • Je prends une entrée et un plat principal.I'll have a starter and a main course.
  • Je voudrais du fromage et de la salade.I would like some cheese and some salad.
  • Une bouteille d'eau, s'il vous plaît.A bottle of water, please.
✅ PART 2 · CHOISIS LA BONNE RÉPLIQUE 12 min

Four moments at the table. Pick the phrase that fits. Instant feedback — practice, not a grade.

✎ ITEM 1
The waiter arrives. How do you ask to order?
✎ ITEM 2
You want the fish as your main dish. What do you say?
✎ ITEM 3
You've finished and want to pay. What do you ask for?
✎ ITEM 4
You want a bottle of water. Which is correct?
✍️ PART 3 · À TOI DE COMMANDER 8 min

Write your own order

In your notebook, write a four-line order: (1) ask to order, (2) a starter, (3) a main course with a partitive or quantity, (4) a drink. Use je voudrais or je prends.

Model: Bonjour ! Je voudrais commander, s'il vous plaît. Comme entrée, je prends de la soupe. Comme plat principal, je prends le poisson avec des légumes. Et une bouteille d'eau, s'il vous plaît.

Keep this — you'll reuse it for your Speaking Task on Day 7. Check every partitive and quantity before you move on.

🎧 PART 4 · LA DICTÉE 10 min

Hear it, type it, check it

Play each clip (as many times as you need). Type exactly what you hear — accents and all — then press Check. Use Reveal only after you have tried. Don't reveal early; the struggle is the learning.

Accents: — click a box first, then a letter.
1Play, type, then check.
Je voudrais commander, s'il vous plaît.
2Play, type, then check.
Je prends une entrée et un plat principal.
3Listen for two partitives (du … de la).
Je voudrais du fromage et de la salade.
4A quantity — catch the d' before eau.
Une bouteille d'eau, s'il vous plaît.
5Third person + partitive.
Elle prend de la soupe.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +10 min
Spiral to Semester 1's food words (Module 7): write four more lines ordering someone else's meal — mon ami prend du pain, ma sœur prend une tranche de gâteau… Using prend for a named person is the most common slip; practice it now.
✏️ Accord & conjugaison — entraînement
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
PRENDRE + PARTITIVES 12 min
Seven items on Module 3’s grammar: conjugating prendre, and choosing the partitive that matches the noun.
✎ ITEM 1
Nous ______ le menu du jour (the menu of the day). (prendre)
✎ ITEM 2
Je ______ une salade comme entrée. (prendre)
✎ ITEM 3
Mes parents ______ du poisson. (prendre)
✎ ITEM 4
Je voudrais ______ eau, s’il vous plaît.
✎ ITEM 5
Elle mange ______ viande. (f.)
✎ ITEM 6

✎ ITEM 7
Nous prenons ______ légumes. (pl.)
⚡ Défi éclair
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
BUILD IT YOURSELF 5 min
Build each one in your head — or out loud — before you reveal the answer.
⚡ DÉFI 1
Order it in French: I’ll have the fish with vegetables.
Je prends du poisson avec des légumes.
du for a masculine noun, des for the plural.
⚡ DÉFI 2
Say it in French: We would like a bottle of water.
Nous voudrions une bouteille d’eau.
After a quantity word, the partitive drops to plain de.
⚡ DÉFI 3
Ask for the bill, politely.
L’addition, s’il vous plaît.
The standard phrase — no verb needed.
