TODAY'S FOCUS
You have the words, the verb, and the quantities. Today you run the whole scene — from sitting down to asking for the bill.
The ordering sequence is a fixed little dance: greet, ask for the menu, order with Je voudrais… or Je prends…, and close with l'addition, s'il vous plaît. Then you'll write your own order and listen to a dictation and write down what you hear. You'll close with two short practice rounds on prendre, the partitive and the polite order.

ILA SÉQUENCE POUR COMMANDER10 min

The order of ordering

The polite formula. Je voudrais… (“I would like…”) is softer than Je veux (“I want”) — always use je voudrais or je prends with the waiter. Close with L'addition, s'il vous plaît.
Ordering for the table. When you order for more than yourself, je voudrais becomes nous voudrions (“we would like”): Nous voudrions deux cafés, s'il vous plaît. (You'll meet the whole verb in Module 9 — for now, just these two.)

Listen to the pieces of a real order:

  • Je voudrais commander, s'il vous plaît.I would like to order, please.
  • Je prends une entrée et un plat principal.I'll have a starter and a main course.
  • Je voudrais du fromage et de la salade.I would like some cheese and some salad.
  • Une bouteille d'eau, s'il vous plaît.A bottle of water, please.

One person: prend. Several people: prennent. Nous: prenons. And after a quantity word: plain de / d’.
