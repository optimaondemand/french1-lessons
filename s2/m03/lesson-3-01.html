Lesson 3.01
⚜ Au restaurant — Restaurant Basics
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
In France, a meal is never just food. What does the French table say about how the French live?
This module takes you inside a French restaurant. Today you meet the ten words you need to sit down, read a menu, and follow a meal from start to finish — from l'entrée to l'addition. You will hear each one spoken before you try it, because a menu you can hear is a menu you can read.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce ten core restaurant words, from la carte to l'addition.
2
I will be able to name the courses of a French meal in order by ear.
📋 WHAT YOU NEED FOR THIS LESSON
▶
Headphones or speakers — today is a listening day.
▶
Your vocabulary notebook or flashcard app.
⏰ PART 1 · METTONS-NOUS À TABLE 12 min

First, a quick look back

Back in Semester 1 (Module 7) you learned food words: le pain, le fromage, une pomme. This module takes that vocabulary out to dinner — into a real restaurant, with courses, quantities, and a waiter to order from. Warm up by naming three foods you already know in French, out loud.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
When you eat out, which part of the meal matters most to you?
📚 PART 2 · LE VOCABULAIRE DU RESTAURANT 18 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 on each word and say it back out loud. These ten walk you through a whole meal — the room, the menu, the courses, the drink, the waiter, the bill. Notice l'entrée is the starter, not the main dish (a classic false friend !).

  • le restaurantthe restaurant
  • le menuthe set menu
  • la cartethe menu (à la carte)
  • l’entréethe starter / appetizer
  • le plat principalthe main course
  • le dessertthe dessert
  • la boissonthe drink / beverage
  • le serveurthe waiter
  • l’additionthe check / bill
  • commanderto order
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Go down the list a second time, but this time shadow each word: play it, pause, and repeat it out loud three times before moving on. Shadowing is the single fastest way to make new sounds feel natural.
📝 PART 3 · RÉVISE TON VOCABULAIRE 10 min

Drill the words with the Vocabulaire page

You don’t have to build your word bank from scratch. Open the Module 3 Vocabulaire study page (in this module) — every word, its meaning, and its audio in one place, as flip cards you can drill by ear. Because your Vocabulary Check is listening-based, practice by hearing the word and recalling its meaning.

Making your own paper or digital cards on top of that is a great memory booster — but it’s optional, not required.
