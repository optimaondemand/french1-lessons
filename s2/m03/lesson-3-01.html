Au restaurant

Restaurant Basics · about 50 minutes

TODAY'S FOCUS
In France, a meal is never just food. What does the French table say about how the French live?
This module takes you inside a French restaurant. Today you meet the ten words you need to sit down, read a menu, and follow a meal from start to finish — from l'entrée (the starter) to l'addition (the bill). You will hear each one spoken before you try it, because a menu you can hear is a menu you can read. To finish, you'll open the Carte de France and start learning the regions — each one famous for its own dishes.
BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce ten core restaurant words, from la carte (the menu) to l'addition (the bill).
2
I will be able to name the courses of a French meal in order by ear.
3
I will be able to find France's 13 metropolitan regions on the interactive map.

IMETTONS-NOUS À TABLE12 min

First, a quick look back

Back in Semester 1 (Module 7) you learned food words: le pain, le fromage, une pomme. This module takes that vocabulary out to dinner — into a real restaurant, with courses, quantities, and a waiter to order from. Warm up by naming three foods you already know in French, out loud.

IILE VOCABULAIRE DU RESTAURANT18 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 on each word and say it back out loud. These ten walk you through a whole meal — the room, the menu, the courses, the drink, the waiter, the bill. Notice l'entrée is the starter, not the main dish (a classic false friend !).

  • le restaurantthe restaurant
  • le menuthe set menu
  • la cartethe menu (à la carte)
  • l’entréethe starter / appetizer
  • le plat principalthe main course
  • le dessertthe dessert
  • la boissonthe drink / beverage
  • le serveurthe waiter
  • l’additionthe check / bill
  • commanderto order

⭐ Extra practice for this part is in Pour aller plus loin, at the end of the lesson.

IIIRÉVISE TON VOCABULAIRE10 min

Drill the words with the Vocabulaire page

You don’t have to build your word bank from scratch. Open the Module 3 Vocabulaire study page (in this module) — every word, its meaning, and its audio in one place, as flip cards you can drill by ear. Making your own digital flash cards on top of that is a great memory booster — but it’s optional, not required.

IVEntraînement · practice, not graded
La Carte de France
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
EXPLORER + QUIZ MODES 13 min
  • Explorer mode: hover and click to learn the 13 metropolitan regions — Île-de-France, Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, and the rest.
  • Quiz mode: timed click-to-find. Your score, errors, and time are shown — restart as often as you like.
  • Tie it to this module: France's regions are famous for their spécialités (specialty dishes) — the cassoulet of the Southwest, the crêpes of Brittany, the bouillabaisse of Provence. As you place each region, name one dish you'd like to taste there.
  • Mastering all 13 prepares you for the Carte de France extra-credit challenge in Module 10, the final review.
