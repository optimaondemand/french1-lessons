📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
How do we compare and celebrate — in music and in life?
Welcome to the last full theme of French 1: music and celebration. In Module 7 you learned to refer to things. This module you'll learn to refer to the people you sing to, give gifts to, and invite — and to compare one song, concert, or performer with another. Today you meet the ten words that fill a French party: songs, singers, concerts, birthdays, and gifts. You'll hear each one spoken before you try it. To finish, you'll open the Carte de France and review its regions.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce ten French words for music and celebration.
2
I will be able to match each spoken word to its meaning by ear.
3
I will be able to locate the 13 metropolitan regions of France on the map.

⏰ PART 1 · SE METTRE EN ROUTE 12 min

First, a quick look back

In Module 7 you learned to replace things with pronouns (je le vois, je les aime). This module goes one step further: you'll learn to say to whom you give and speak — and to compare. Warm up by thinking of a party in French: une fête, la musique, un cadeau (a gift).

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
What is your favorite way to celebrate?
📚 PART 2 · LE VOCABULAIRE DE LA FÊTE 18 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 on each word and say it back out loud. These ten words describe the heart of a celebration — the songs, the people who perform them, and the gifts. Notice le chanteur / la chanteuse: the same job, one form for a man, one for a woman.

  • la chansonthe song
  • le chanteurthe singer (m.)
  • la chanteusethe singer (f.)
  • le concertthe concert
  • la fêtethe party / celebration
  • l’anniversairethe birthday
  • le cadeauthe gift
  • danserto dance
  • chanterto sing
  • offrirto give (a gift)
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Go down the list a second time, but this time shadow each word: play it, pause, and repeat it out loud three times before moving on. Notice that chanter (to sing) and la chanson (the song) share a root — a family of words you already half-know.
📝 PART 3 · RÉVISE TON VOCABULAIRE 10 min

Drill the words with the Vocabulaire page

You don’t have to build your word bank from scratch. Open the Module 8 Vocabulaire study page (in this module) — every word, its meaning, and its audio in one place, as flip cards you can drill by ear. Because your Vocabulary Check is listening-based, practice by hearing the word and recalling its meaning.

Making your own paper or digital cards on top of that is a great memory booster — but it’s optional, not required.
