Lesson 8.03
⚜ Les comparatifs — More, Less, As… and the Two “Betters”
Lesson · about 50 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
One pattern lets you compare almost anything — and two little words trip up nearly every learner. Today you master both.
Rule 1 was lui and leur. Today: the comparative. plus…que (more than), moins…que (less than), aussi…que (as…as). Then the pair that everyone confuses: mieux (better, an adverb — for how you do something) and meilleur (better, an adjective — for a noun). You'll finish with seven quick practice items that mix lui / leur with the new comparisons.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to compare two things with plus…que, moins…que, and aussi…que.
2
I will be able to choose between mieux (adverb) and meilleur (adjective) correctly.
3
I will be able to use lui / leur and build comparisons in short practice items.
⚖️ PART 1 · PLUS, MOINS, AUSSI… QUE 12 min

The comparison sandwich

The pattern: plus / moins / aussi + adjective + que (an adverb can take the place of the adjective). Put the describing word in the middle and que (than / as) before the second thing. Cette chanson est plus belle que l'autre. (This song is more beautiful than the other one.) Two new words in the examples: l'autre (the other one) and the adverb bien (well) — aussi bien que (as well as).

Listen — the adjective agrees with its noun, but the frame never changes:

  • Cette chanson est plus belle que l'autre.This song is more beautiful than the other one.
  • Ce concert est moins cher que le concert de samedi.This concert is less expensive than Saturday's concert.
  • Elle chante aussi bien que son frère.She sings as well as her brother.
⭐ PART 2 · MIEUX vs MEILLEUR 10 min

Two words that both mean “better”

The rule. mieux is an adverb — it describes a verb (how well you do something): il chante mieux. meilleur is an adjective — it describes a noun: une meilleure chanson. English uses “better” for both; French keeps them apart. Put le or la in front and it means “the best”: le meilleur (the best) concert, la meilleure (the best) chanson.

Listen to mieux describing how he plays — it attaches to the verb jouer:

  • Il joue de la guitare mieux que moi.He plays guitar better than me.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
A quick test you can use forever: if “better” sits next to a verb (sings better, plays better), use mieux. If it sits next to a noun (a better song, the best singer), use meilleur(e). Write one sentence of each kind about your favorite artist.
