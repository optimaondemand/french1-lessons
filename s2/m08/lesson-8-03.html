Lesson 8.03
⚜ Les comparatifs — More, Less, As… and the Two “Betters”
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
One pattern lets you compare almost anything — and two little words trip up nearly every learner. Today you master both.
Rule 1 was lui and leur. Today: the comparative. plus…que (more than), moins…que (less than), aussi…que (as…as). Then the pair that everyone confuses: mieux (better, an adverb — for how you do something) and meilleur (better, an adjective — for a noun).
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to compare two things with plus…que, moins…que, and aussi…que.
2
I will be able to choose between mieux (adverb) and meilleur (adjective) correctly.
⚖️ PART 1 · PLUS, MOINS, AUSSI… QUE 12 min

The comparison sandwich

The pattern: plus / moins / aussi + adjective + que. Put the describing word in the middle and que (than / as) before the second thing. Cette chanson est plus belle que l'autre.

Listen — the adjective agrees with its noun, but the frame never changes:

  • Cette chanson est plus belle que l'autre.more… than
  • Ce concert est moins cher que le concert de samedi.less… than
  • Elle chante aussi bien que son frère.as… as
⭐ PART 2 · MIEUX vs MEILLEUR 10 min

Two words that both mean “better”

The rule. mieux is an adverb — it describes a verb (how well you do something): il chante mieux. meilleur is an adjective — it describes a noun: une meilleure chanson. English uses “better” for both; French keeps them apart.

Listen to mieux describing how he plays — it attaches to the verb jouer:

  • Il joue de la guitare mieux que moi.He plays guitar better than me.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
A quick test you can use forever: if “better” sits next to a verb (sings better, plays better), use mieux. If it sits next to a noun (a better song, the best singer), use meilleur(e). Write one sentence of each kind about your favorite artist.

✍️ PART 4 · À TON TOUR 8 min

Compare two singers. In your notebook, write three sentences about Ana and Louis: use plus…que, then moins…que, then mieux que. Commit to an answer first, then reveal to check — trying before you see the answer is what makes it stick.

🧠 COMPARE · Ana & Louis
Write your three comparison sentences, then reveal a model.
Ana est plus célèbre que Louis.Ana is more famous than Louis.
Louis est moins populaire que Ana.Louis is less popular than Ana.
Ana chante mieux que Louis.Ana sings better than Louis.
Notice the last one uses mieux (next to the verb chante), not meilleur. If you kept that straight, you've got today's hardest point.
