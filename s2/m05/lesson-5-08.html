Grammar Quiz · Le passé composé avec être
30 pts · 2 attempts
COMPLETED IN CANVAS · AUTO-GRADED · should be submitted by the end of the module 30 min
  • Past-tense listening items (~15 pts): hear a sentence, then answer — identify the subject, the agreement, affirmative vs. negative, or the meaning. Headphones recommended; you can replay each clip.
  • Grammar items (~15 pts): choose or supply the correct form of être, apply the past-participle agreement, place negation, and tell être verbs from avoir verbs.
  • Two attempts — your higher score counts. Review any misses before your second try.
▶ To take the quiz: return to Module 5 in Canvas and open the Grammar Quiz — it is in the module’s item list, below the eight lessons.
IIIRÉVISE TES ERREURS5 min

After your first attempt, look at what you missed. Most Module 5 errors fall into three buckets: wrong helper (avoir where you needed être), missing agreement (forgetting the -e or -s), or misplaced negation. Name which one caught you, fix that one thing, and take your second attempt.

IVAU REVOIR, CAILLEBOTTE 5 minpractice · not graded

Before you close Module 5, go back to Caillebotte’s painting one last time. You will compose three sentences about it in the passé composé with être, using the tools below: when and where the people went, where one person went, and one thing they did not do. The fiche (fact card) gives you the words for the painting, the word bank adds more, and the coach checks each sentence as you write. Your sentences are saved in All Your Work.

CLÔTURE DU MODULE 5 — CE QUE TU SAIS FAIRE
  • I can name and understand 20 city & movement words by ear.
  • I can form the passé composé with être (the DR MRS VANDERTRAMP verbs).
  • I can make the past participle agree with the subject (allé / allée / allés / allées).
  • I can negate an être verb (je ne suis pas sorti) and tell être from avoir.
  • I can narrate a trip through a city in the past, in writing and out loud.
💡 Heads up — Module 6 builds on everything so far. You’ll use both halves of the passé composé (avoir and être) all through Module 6, so keep your être table and your Module 4 avoir table fresh.
