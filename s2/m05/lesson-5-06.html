📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Graded work opens tomorrow. Today is the tune-up — by ear, by pen, and by the table.
You've met everything Module 5 asks of you. This is the day to make it automatic: quiz yourself by ear (that's how the Vocabulary Check works), narrate a trip of your own, and run the être conjugation table one more time. We'll also spiral back to avoir (Module 4) — because the quiz will ask you to tell the two helpers apart.

✍️ PART 2 · TON TRAJET EN VILLE 15 min

Write five sentences narrating a trip through a city

Tell the story in order. Use at least five être verbs, one negative, one transport word, and one landmark. This is your rehearsal for both the Speaking Task and Langue en action — write it well and reuse it.

Checklist before you stop:  □ right helper (être) on every movement verb  □ agreement ending matches the subject  □ ne…pas wraps the helper  □ a transport word  □ a landmark.
📋 PART 3 · LE TABLEAU, ENCORE UNE FOIS 13 min

From memory, conjugate rentrer (to return home) in the passé composé through all six persons, with agreement. Commit first — then reveal.

🧠 CONJUGATE · rentrer (passé composé)
je suis rentré(e)nous sommes rentré(e)s
tu es rentré(e)vous êtes rentré(e)(s)
il est rentré / elle est rentréeils sont rentrés / elles sont rentrées
If all six agreements matched, you're ready for the assessments. If not, run the table twice more before Day 7.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +10 min · cumulative spiral (Module 4)
Tell them apart. Write four sentences: two with an être verb (je suis allé, elle est arrivée) and two with an avoir verb from Module 4 (j'ai vu la place, nous avons pris le bus). Then underline the participle in each and note: it agrees with the subject only in the être sentences. Telling the two helpers apart is exactly what tomorrow's items test.
