TODAY'S FOCUS
You know the helper and the agreement. Today you add the negative — then narrate a whole trip.
First, how to say you didn't go: with être, ne…pas wraps around the helper (je ne suis pas sorti). Then three moves: choose the right form, write your own trip through the city, and listen to a dictation and write down what you hear — the bridge between hearing French and producing it, and exactly what your listening-based checks reward. To finish, you'll type past-tense forms — helper, agreement and negation — with every accent in place.
BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…

I will be able to negate an être verb (ne…pas around the helper) and write my own trip narration.
2
I will be able to understand a spoken passé-composé sentence well enough to write it down.
3
I will be able to type the passé composé of an être verb correctly, with agreement, negation and accents.
ILA NÉGATION AVEC ÊTRE8 min

Saying what you didn't do

The rule. ne and pas wrap around the helper (être), not the participle: je ne suis pas sorti. The participle stays outside the sandwich, on the right.

You met ne…pas in Module 2, and again with avoir in Module 4. Same idea — it just wraps whichever helper the verb uses. Listen:

  • Je ne suis pas sorti hier soir.I didn't go out last night.

IIIRACONTE TON TRAJET10 min

Write four sentences narrating a trip through a city

Narrate a real or imagined trip through a city in the past, one sentence in each box, using four être verbs. Make one of them negative (ne…pas) and use at least one transport word and one landmark. Click a word in the word bank to add it. Writing as a girl or woman? Add -e: je suis allée.

The coach ticks each rule as you write and suggests a fix when the helper, the agreement or the negation looks wrong. Keep these sentences: you will reuse them for your Speaking Task on Day 7.

IVLA DICTÉE10 min

Hear it, type it, check it

Play each clip (as many times as you need). Type exactly what you hear — accents and agreement endings and all — then press Check. Use Reveal only after you have tried. Don't reveal early; the struggle is the learning.

Accents: — click a box first, then a letter.
1Play, type, then check.

2Play, type, then check.

3Listen for the feminine ending (-e).
Elle est partie à huit heures.
4A plural subject — catch the ending.
Nous sommes sortis en ville.
5A negative sentence — catch the ne…pas.
Je ne suis pas sorti hier soir.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +10 min
Spiral to Module 4 (avoir): two être sentences and two avoir sentences. Type each line in French, then press Check. Notice the participle does not agree with the subject after avoir — that contrast is the single most common slip.
🧠 ÊTRE OR AVOIR ?
I went downtown. (a boy speaking)
I took the bus.
She arrived late.
We saw the bridge.
Accents are part of the word. Click in a box, then click a letter to add it.
1. Je suis allé au centre-ville.I went downtown. (a boy speaking)
2. J’ai pris le bus.I took the bus.
3. Elle est arrivée tard.She arrived late.
4. Nous avons vu le pont.We saw the bridge.

Movement verbs take être and agree (allé, arrivée); prendre and voir take avoir, and pris, vu never change.
