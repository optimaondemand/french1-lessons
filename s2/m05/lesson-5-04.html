Lesson 5.04
⚜ Négation & récit — Narrating a Trip Through the City
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You know the helper and the agreement. Today you add the negative — then narrate a whole trip.
First, how to say you didn't go: with être, ne…pas wraps around the helper (je ne suis pas sorti). Then three moves: choose the right form, write your own trip through the city, and take an audio dictation — the bridge between hearing French and producing it, and exactly what your listening-based checks reward.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to negate an être verb (ne…pas around the helper) and write my own trip narration.
2
I will be able to understand a spoken passé-composé sentence well enough to write it down.
🚫 PART 1 · LA NÉGATION AVEC ÊTRE 8 min

Saying what you didn't do

The rule. ne and pas wrap around the helper (être), not the participle: je ne suis pas sorti. The participle stays outside the sandwich, on the right.

You met ne…pas in Module 2, and again with avoir in Module 4. Same idea — it just wraps whichever helper the verb uses. Listen:

  • Je ne suis pas sorti hier soir.I didn't go out last night.
✅ PART 2 · CHOISIS LA BONNE FORME 12 min

Four sentences. Pick the form that fits — the helper, the agreement, or the negation. Instant feedback; practice, not a grade.

✎ ITEM 1
Je ______ allé au centre-ville en bus.
✎ ITEM 2
Marie est ______ du bus à l'arrêt. (descendre)
✎ ITEM 3
Which sentence is correctly negated?
✎ ITEM 4
Les filles sont ______ sur le pont. (tomber)
✍️ PART 3 · RACONTE TON TRAJET 10 min

Write four sentences narrating a trip through a city

In your notebook, narrate a real or imagined trip through a city in the past, using four être verbs. Make one of them negative (ne…pas) and use at least one transport word and one landmark.

Model: Je suis allé au centre-ville en tramway. Je suis monté sur le pont pour voir la place. Je ne suis pas rentré à pied — j'ai pris le bus. Je suis arrivé à la maison à six heures.

Keep these — you'll reuse them for your Speaking Task on Day 8. Check each verb: right helper? right agreement? negation wrapping être?

🎧 PART 4 · LA DICTÉE 10 min

Hear it, write it, check it

Play each clip (as many times as you need). Write down exactly what you hear — accents and agreement endings and all — then reveal to check. Don't reveal early; the struggle is the learning.

1Play, write, then reveal.
Je suis allé au centre-ville.
2Play, write, then reveal.
Le train est arrivé à l'heure.
3Listen for the feminine ending (-e).
Elle est partie à huit heures.
4A plural subject — catch the ending.
Nous sommes sortis en ville.
5A negative sentence — catch the ne…pas.
Je ne suis pas sorti hier soir.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +10 min
Spiral to Module 4 (avoir): rewrite two of your trip sentences using an avoir verb instead (j'ai vu la place, j'ai pris le bus). Notice the participle does not agree with the subject there — that contrast is the single most common slip. Practice it now.
