Le passé composé avec être

The Verbs of Movement · about 55 minutes
Le tableau du module : Gustave Caillebotte, Rue de Paris, temps de pluie (Paris Street; Rainy Day), 1877
TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you learned to move through the city. Today you learn to say you did — with a brand-new helper verb.
You'll finish the vocabulary with more movement verbs and city landmarks, then meet the idea that runs the whole module: a special group of verbs forms the past tense with être, not avoir. They're the verbs of coming and going — and there's a famous name to help you remember them: DR MRS VANDERTRAMP. You'll finish with a short ear-quiz — hear a word, choose its meaning — the exact skill your Vocabulary Check rewards.

IILA GRAMMAIRE — LE PASSÉ COMPOSÉ AVEC ÊTRE15 min

A different helper for verbs of coming and going

In Module 4 you built the past with avoir: j'ai mangé. But a small, important group of verbs — almost all describing movement or a change of state — uses être as the helper instead: je suis allé (I went), not j'ai allé. To remember which verbs, learn the name DR MRS VANDERTRAMP.

🚌 THE ÊTRE VERBS · DR MRS VANDERTRAMP
  • Devenir — to become
  • Revenir — to come back
  • Monter — to go up
  • Rester — to stay
  • Sortir — to go out
  • Venir — to come
  • Aller — to go
  • Naître — to be born
  • Descendre — to go down
  • Entrer — to enter
  • Rentrer — to return home
  • Tomber — to fall
  • Retourner — to return
  • Arriver — to arrive
  • Mourir — to die
  • Partir — to leave

Now the pattern. Take être in the present, then add the past participle. Here is aller (to go) — the model être verb:

SubjectêtrePassé composé of aller
jesuisje suis allé(e)
tuestu es allé(e)
il / elle / onestil est allé / elle est allée
noussommesnous sommes allé(e)s
vousêtesvous êtes allé(e)(s)
ils / ellessontils sont allés / elles sont allées
-re verbs → -u. Verbs ending in -re make their past participle in -u: descendre → descendu (il est descendu, elle est descendue).

Hear three of them in action. (You'll learn why the ending changes — allé / allée — tomorrow.)

  • Je suis allé au centre-ville.I went downtown.
  • Le train est arrivé à l'heure.The train arrived on time.
  • Il est resté à la maison.He stayed home.
