Lesson 7.02
⚜ Les repas & le partitif — Meals + Saying “Some”
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you named the food. Today you learn the words for meals — and how to say you're eating some of it.
You'll pick up the three French meals, the verbs manger and boire, and the polite je voudrais. Then you meet the idea at the heart of this module: the partitive article. When you eat an unmeasured amount of something — not one, not all — French says du, de la, de l', des: “some.”
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce the words for meals and ordering (words 12–22).
2
I will be able to explain what the partitive means and choose du / de la / de l' / des by the noun's gender and number.

🧠 PART 2 · LE PARTITIF — COMMENT DIRE « SOME » ? 17 min

“Some” food — du, de la, de l', des

When you eat or drink an uncountable amount — not one apple, not the bread, but some — French can't just drop the article the way English does. It uses the partitive, and the form matches the gender and number of the food.

Noun is…PartitiveExample
masculine (le)duje mange du pain
feminine (la)de laje voudrais de la viande
before a vowel / h (l')de l'elle boit de l'eau
plural (les)desil mange des légumes

Hear all four. Notice how the little word changes with the food it points to:

  • Je mange du pain.I eat (some) bread.
  • Je voudrais de la viande.I would like (some) meat.
  • Elle boit de l’eau.She drinks (some) water.
  • Il mange des légumes.He eats (some) vegetables.
✅ PART 3 · VÉRIFIE TA COMPRÉHENSION 10 min

Pick the partitive that matches the food. You get instant feedback — this is practice, not a grade.
