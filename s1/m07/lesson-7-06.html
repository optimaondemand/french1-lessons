📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Graded work opens tomorrow. Today is the tune-up — by ear, by pen, and by the partitive table.
You've met everything Module 7 asks of you. This is the day to make it automatic: quiz yourself by ear (that's how the Vocabulary Check works), write a real day of eating and drinking, and run the partitive one more time so it's cold before Day 7.

✍️ PART 2 · TA JOURNÉE À TABLE 15 min

Write five sentences about what you eat and drink

Walk through your day: petit-déjeuner, déjeuner, dîner. Use at least five foods with the partitive, one negative (je ne mange pas de…), and one polite request (je voudrais…). This is your rehearsal for both the Speaking Task and Langue en action — write it well and reuse it.

Checklist before you stop:  □ right partitive for each food's gender  □ de l' before a vowel (de l'eau)  □ negative flattens to de  □ a polite je voudrais  □ a meal named (petit-déjeuner / déjeuner / dîner).
📋 PART 3 · LE PARTITIF, ENCORE UNE FOIS 13 min

From memory, give the partitive for each food — both affirmative and negative. Commit first — then reveal.

🧠 LE PARTITIF · affirmative & negative
pain (m.)je mange du painje ne mange pas de pain
viande (f.)je mange de la viandeje ne mange pas de viande
eau (vowel)je bois de l'eauje ne bois pas d'eau
fruits (pl.)je mange des fruitsje ne mange pas de fruits
If every form matched, you're ready for the assessments. If not, run the table twice more before Day 7.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +10 min · cumulative spiral
Mix Module 7 with earlier words: write four sentences that each combine a food (with its partitive) and vocabulary from Modules 1–6 — a family member, a time, a place, a leisure plan (le samedi, ma sœur mange du fromage au parc). Interleaving old and new is what makes review actually stick.
