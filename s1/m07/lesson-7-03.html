Lesson 7.03
⚜ Je voudrais & the Negative — Ordering Politely, Refusing Cleanly
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Two moves turn the partitive into real French: ordering politely, and one clean rule for saying no.
Yesterday: “some” matches the food's gender. Today: the polite je voudrais that softens any request, and the surprise rule — after a negative, all four partitives collapse into a single de (je ne mange pas de viande). Then you'll try it yourself before you see the answer.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to use je voudrais to order politely and place the partitive after it.
2
I will be able to turn any partitive into de after a negative and choose the right form across a full set of foods.
🍽️ PART 1 · « JE VOUDRAIS » — ORDER POLITELY 10 min

The polite way to ask

Je voudrais means “I would like.” It's softer and more polite than je veux (“I want”) — the phrase you'll use at every café and bakery. Add s'il vous plaît and you sound like a local.

After je voudrais, use un / une for one whole item, or the partitive for “some.” Listen:

  • Je voudrais un café, s’il vous plaît.I would like a coffee, please.
  • Je voudrais de la viande.I would like (some) meat.
🚫 PART 2 · LE PARTITIF APRÈS UNE NÉGATION 10 min

Saying you don't eat something

The rule. After a negative (ne…pas), every partitive — du, de la, de l', des — becomes a single de (or d' before a vowel). The gender stops mattering the moment the sentence turns negative.

Je mange du pain  →  Je ne mange pas de pain.   Je bois de l'eau  →  Je ne bois pas d'eau. Listen:

  • Je ne mange pas de viande.I don’t eat meat.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Spiral back to Module 2: there, ne…pas simply made a verb negative (je n'aime pas). Here it also transforms the article that follows. Write four pairs — an affirmative partitive sentence and its negative — and watch every du / de la / de l' / des flatten to de.
📋 PART 3 · LE TABLEAU COMPLET — LE PARTITIF 12 min

The whole system on one page

Read each row aloud. The last column is the twist you just learned: everything becomes de in the negative.

NounAffirmativeNegative
le pain (m.)du painne…pas de pain
la viande (f.)de la viandene…pas de viande
l'eau (vowel)de l'eaune…pas d'eau
les légumes (pl.)des légumesne…pas de légumes

One more thing to hear: boire is irregular. You'll use je bois, il boit, and especially nous buvons. Listen:

  • Nous buvons du jus le matin.We drink juice in the morning.
✍️ PART 4 · À TON TOUR 8 min

On paper or in your head, fill the correct affirmative partitive for each food: Je mange ___ . Commit to an answer first, then reveal to check. Trying before you see the answer is what makes it stick.

🧠 LE PARTITIF · six foods
Give the partitive for: pain · viande · eau · fruits · café · poisson. Then reveal.
je mange du painje voudrais de la viande
je bois de l'eauje mange des fruits
je bois du caféje mange du poisson
Masculine → du, feminine → de la, vowel → de l', plural → des. If you matched all six, you've got the partitive cold — now try each one in the negative and watch it become de.
