Lesson 8.04
⚜ Mise en pratique — Use the Whole Semester
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You have the map. Today you use it — choosing structures, writing your own Paris sentences, and writing down what you hear.
Three moves, each harder than the last: pick the right structure, write your own sentences about Paris, then take an audio dictation that spans all seven review sentences. Dictation is the bridge between hearing French and producing it — exactly what your listening-based checks reward.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to choose the correct S1 structure in a full sentence and write my own Paris sentences.
2
I will be able to understand a spoken review sentence well enough to write it down.
✅ PART 1 · CHOISIS LA BONNE STRUCTURE 12 min

Four sentences drawn from across the semester. Pick the form that fits. Instant feedback — practice, not a grade.

✎ ITEM 1 · aller + infinitive
Demain, nous ______ visiter Notre-Dame.
✎ ITEM 2 · partitive
Au jardin, on mange ______ pain et ______ fruits.
✎ ITEM 3 · reflexive
On ______ promène le long de la Seine.
✎ ITEM 4 · question
Which is a correctly formed question?
✍️ PART 2 · À TOI D'ÉCRIRE 13 min

Write three sentences about a day in Paris

In your notebook, write three sentences about what you would do in Paris, using Module 8 vocabulary. Make one use aller + infinitive, one use a partitive, and one be a question.

Model: D'abord, nous allons visiter la tour Eiffel. Ensuite, on mange du pain et des croissants dans un jardin. Est-ce que tu veux découvrir le musée du Louvre ?

Keep these — you'll reuse them for your Speaking Task on Day 8. Check each sentence against the grammar map from Day 3 before you move on.

🎧 PART 3 · LA DICTÉE 15 min

Hear it, write it, check it

Seven clips — one per S1 structure. Play each (as many times as you need), write down exactly what you hear (accents and all), then reveal to check. Don't reveal early; the struggle is the learning.

1être — play, write, then reveal.
Je suis fier d’être ici.
2avoir — play, write, then reveal.
Paris a beaucoup de musées.
3aller + infinitive — listen for the two verbs.
Nous allons visiter la tour Eiffel.
4reflexive — catch the pronoun se.
On se promène le long de la Seine.
5partitive — listen for du and des.
On mange du pain et des croissants.
6time — catch the hour.
Le musée ouvre à neuf heures.
7question — a full est-ce que question.
Est-ce que tu veux visiter le Louvre ?
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +10 min
Spiral across the whole semester: pick any three modules (M1–M7) and write one sentence each that reuses their structure but talks about Paris. Interleaving old material with new content is exactly what the cumulative final rewards.
