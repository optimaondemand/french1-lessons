⚡ Défi éclair
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
BUILD IT YOURSELF 5 min
Build each one in your head — or out loud — before you reveal it.
⚡ DÉFI 1
Say it in French: We are going to visit the Eiffel Tower.
Nous allons visiter la tour Eiffel.
aller + infinitive, exactly as in Module 6.
⚡ DÉFI 2
Say it in French: Paris is the capital of France.
Paris est la capitale de la France.
être for what something is, and la France keeps its article.
⚡ DÉFI 3
Say it in French: I am French.
Je suis français. / Je suis française.
française with the -e if you are female. Both are correct.
