⚜ Grammar Quiz (Cumulative) & Semester Close — the Gate
Assessment day · about 40 minutes
The last graded item of Semester 1 opens today — and it is cumulative. This Grammar Quiz spans the whole semester, not just Module 8. Warm up your ear first (it includes listening questions), then take it in Canvas. Two attempts are allowed.
🎧 PART 1 · ÉCHAUFFEMENT D'ÉCOUTE 5 min

Get your ear tuned. First, listen to a familiar passage — then answer one comprehension question by ear. This is a warm-up, not the quiz.

Listen. What kind of sentence is this?
Grammar Quiz · Synthèse du semestre
30 pts · 2 attempts
COMPLETED IN CANVAS · AUTO-GRADED · CUMULATIVE 30 min
  • Cumulative across Semester 1: greetings & être (M1) · school & -er verbs (M2) · family & avoir (M3) · schedules, time & questions (M4) · routines & reflexives (M5) · leisure, aller + infinitive & weather (M6) · food & partitives (M7). Framed as the semester synthesis, not only Module 8.
  • Listening items (~15 pts): hear a sentence from across the semester, then answer — identify the structure, the subject, or the meaning. Headphones recommended; you can replay each clip.
  • Grammar items (~15 pts): choose or supply the correct form for each S1 structure.
  • Two attempts — your higher score counts. Review any misses before your second try.
▶ To take the quiz: return to Module 8 in Canvas and open the Grammar Quiz — it is in the module’s item list, below the ten leçons.
🔎 PART 3 · RÉVISE TES ERREURS 5 min

After your first attempt, look at what you missed and match it to a module: a verb form (M1–M3), a time or question (M4), a reflexive pronoun (M5), an aller + infinitive (M6), or a partitive (M7). Fix that one thing, then take your second attempt — and note it for the Final Exam.

🏁 CLÔTURE DU SEMESTRE 1 — CE QUE TU SAIS FAIRE
  • I can greet, introduce myself, and use être (M1).
  • I can talk about school and use regular -er verbs (M2).
  • I can describe my family and use avoir (M3).
  • I can tell time, give a schedule, and ask questions (M4).
  • I can describe my daily routine with reflexive verbs (M5).
  • I can talk about free time and the weather with aller + infinitive (M6).
  • I can order food and use partitives (M7).
  • I can talk about Paris and about being français(e), weaving the whole semester together (M8).
