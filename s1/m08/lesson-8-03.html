📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Everything you learned to build a French sentence this semester — gathered, heard, and used once more.
Yesterday: être and avoir. Today: the other five moves — regular -er verbs, reflexive verbs, aller + infinitive (the near future), partitives (some/any), telling time, and asking questions. No new rules — just a clear map, then a challenge where you commit an answer before you check it.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to name every core S1 structure and recognize it inside a sentence.
2
I will be able to build a correct French sentence from each structure without help.
🗺️ PART 1 · LA CARTE GRAMMATICALE 12 min

Seven structures, one semester

Read each row aloud. This is the whole grammatical spine of French 1.

StructureFromExample
être (to be)M1Je suis fier d'être ici.
regular -er verbsM2Je parle français.
avoir (to have)M3Paris a beaucoup de musées.
telling time + questionsM4Le musée ouvre à neuf heures.

Hear two of them — a regular -er verb and a reflexive verb — reviewed in context:

  • Je parle français.I speak French. (−er verb)
  • On se promène le long de la Seine.We stroll along the Seine. (reflexive)
🍽️ PART 2 · LES DEUX QUI PIÈGENT — PARTITIFS & QUESTIONS 10 min

The two that trip people up

Partitives (M7). “Some / any” changes with the noun: du (masc.), de la (fem.), de l' (before a vowel), des (plural). After a negative it all collapses to de: je ne mange pas de viande.
Questions (M4). Three ways to ask: rising intonation (Tu veux visiter ?), est-ce que (Est-ce que tu veux visiter ?), or inversion (Veux-tu visiter ?).
  • On mange du pain et des croissants.We eat bread and croissants. (partitive)
  • Est-ce que tu veux visiter le Louvre ?Do you want to visit the Louvre? (question)
🕑 PART 3 · ALLER + INFINITIF & L'HEURE 10 min

Planning the day & telling the time

Aller + infinitive (M6). To say what you're going to do, conjugate aller and add a plain infinitive: nous allons visiter = we're going to visit.
Telling time (M4). Il est… + the hour; à + a time says when: à neuf heures = at nine. Add et demie (half past), et quart (quarter past), moins le quart (quarter to).
  • Nous allons visiter la tour Eiffel.We are going to visit the Eiffel Tower. (aller + inf.)
  • Le musée ouvre à neuf heures.The museum opens at nine. (time)
✍️ PART 4 · À TON TOUR 8 min

Build one Paris sentence for each structure below, on paper or in your head. Commit to an answer first, then reveal a model to compare. Trying before you see the answer is what makes it stick.

🧠 BUILD · five structures, one city
Write one sentence about Paris for each: (1) aller + inf., (2) a partitive, (3) a time, (4) a reflexive verb, (5) a question. Then reveal.
aller + inf.Nous allons visiter la tour Eiffel.
partitiveOn mange du pain dans le jardin.
timeLe musée ouvre à neuf heures.
reflexiveOn se promène le long de la Seine.
questionEst-ce que tu veux visiter le Louvre ?
Yours won't match word-for-word — that's fine. Check that each one uses its structure correctly. If all five held, you're ready for the whole semester's grammar.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Combine two structures in one sentence — e.g. a question that uses aller + infinitive (Est-ce que tu vas visiter Notre-Dame ?). Weaving structures together is exactly what the cumulative final exam will ask of you.
