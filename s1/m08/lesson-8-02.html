⚜ Je suis français(e) — Identity + Révision I (être & avoir)
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you toured the city. Today you name the country, the flag, the language — and re-open the two verbs that started it all.
First, the eleven identity words: la France, le drapeau, la culture, français(e). Then the first of three review days. This module has no new grammar — instead you re-weave what you already own. Today: être (Module 1) and avoir (Module 3), the two verbs everything else leans on.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce the eleven identity words (words 12–22).
2
I will be able to choose the correct form of être and avoir for any subject.
📚 PART 1 · L'IDENTITÉ FRANÇAISE 13 min

Eleven more words — listen, then repeat

These are the words of France itself — the country, its capital, its flag, its language and culture, and two verbs of discovery, visiter and découvrir. Click the 🔊 and say each one back.

⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +8 min
Open the Module 8 Vocabulaire page and mix yesterday's landmark words with today's identity words. Flip a card, say the French, then check — but do it out of order, so your ear (not the list position) is doing the work.
🧠 PART 2 · RÉVISION — ÊTRE & AVOIR 17 min

The two verbs that carry everything

Back in Module 1 you learned être (to be); in Module 3, avoir (to have). Every module since has used them. Here they are, side by side — read each row aloud.

Subjectêtre — to beavoir — to have
je / j'suisai

noussommesavons

ils / ellessontont

Hear each verb reviewed inside a Paris sentence:

  • Je suis fier d’être ici.I am proud to be here. (être)
  • Paris a beaucoup de musées.Paris has many museums. (avoir)
✅ PART 3 · VÉRIFIE TA COMPRÉHENSION 10 min

Pick the form that matches the subject. You get instant feedback — this is practice, not a grade.

✎ ITEM 1
Paris ______ la capitale de la France. (être)
✎ ITEM 2
Nous ______ un plan du métro. (avoir)
✎ ITEM 3
Les touristes ______ contents de visiter le Louvre. (être)
