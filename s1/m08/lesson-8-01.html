📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A whole semester of French comes to rest in one city — and this module is the gate you walk through to close it.
Module 8 is your Semester 1 synthesis checkpoint — the gate. There is no new grammar here: instead you gather everything you have built (greetings, school, family, schedules, routines, leisure, food) and use it to talk about Paris and about being français(e). Today you meet the city's landmarks by ear. Hold the essential question in mind all week: What have you learned this semester about French — and about yourself as a language learner?
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce eleven Paris landmark and city words.
2
I will be able to match each spoken landmark to its meaning by ear.
📋 WHAT YOU NEED FOR THIS LESSON
▶
Headphones or speakers — today is a listening day.
▶
Your vocabulary notebook or flashcard app — and, if you like, your notes from Modules 0–7.
⏰ PART 1 · SE METTRE EN ROUTE 12 min

Look how far you've come

A semester ago, French was just sounds. Now you can greet people, describe your family and your school day, tell time, talk about your routine and your free time, and order food. This module asks you to bring all of it to one place — Paris — and to notice what it means to speak, even a little, as a français(e). Because this is the gate, the work here also gets you ready for the cumulative Semester 1 Final Exam.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Looking back over the semester, what surprised you most about learning French?
📚 PART 2 · LE VOCABULAIRE DE PARIS 18 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 on each word and say it back out loud. These are the landmarks and the everyday city words you would need to actually move through Paris — the monuments, the river, the streets, the métro. You'll hear each one before you ever try to say it.

  • ParisParis
  • la tour Eiffelthe Eiffel Tower
  • le Louvrethe Louvre
  • le muséethe museum
  • la Seinethe Seine (river)
  • Notre-DameNotre-Dame
  • le quartierthe neighborhood
  • la ruethe street
  • le métrothe subway / metro
  • la garethe train station
  • le jardinthe garden
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Go down the list a second time, but this time shadow each word: play it, pause, and repeat it out loud three times before moving on. Notice the article — le or la — and say it together with the noun, the way a French speaker always would.
📝 PART 3 · RÉVISE TON VOCABULAIRE 10 min

Drill the words with the Vocabulaire page

Open the Module 8 Vocabulaire study page (in this module) — all 22 capstone words, each with its meaning and its audio, as flip cards you can drill by ear. Because your Vocabulary Check is listening-based, practice by hearing the word and recalling its meaning.

Today, focus on the eleven landmark words above; tomorrow you add the eleven identity words. Making your own cards is a great booster — but optional.
