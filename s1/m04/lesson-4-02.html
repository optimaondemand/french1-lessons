⚜ Les mots interrogatifs & dire l’heure — Question Words + Building a Clock Time
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you named the parts of the day. Today you finish the vocabulary and learn to build any clock time in French.
You'll pick up the words a school day runs on — la récréation, la pause déjeuner, commencer, finir — and the question words (quand, pourquoi, combien). Then you meet the one pattern that unlocks the module: Il est ___ heure(s), and how to add et demie, et quart, and moins le quart.

🧠 PART 2 · LA GRAMMAIRE — QUELLE HEURE EST-IL ? 17 min

Building a clock time

To answer Quelle heure est-il ? you start with Il est + the hour + heure(s). One o'clock is une heure (singular); everything else is plural (deux heures). Then you add the minutes: et quart (:15), et demie (:30), or moins le quart (:45, said as “quarter to” the next hour). And two hours have their own names: midi (noon) and minuit (midnight).

ClockFrenchMeaning
8:00Il est huit heuresIt's eight o'clock
8:15Il est huit heures et quartquarter past eight
8:30Il est huit heures et demiehalf past eight
8:45Il est neuf heures moins le quartquarter to nine
12:00Il est midiIt's noon
00:00Il est minuitIt's midnight

Hear the four you'll use most. Notice the little words at the end changing the time:

  • Il est huit heures.It is eight o'clock.
  • Il est huit heures et demie.It is half past eight.
  • Il est neuf heures et quart.It is quarter past nine.
  • Il est midi.It is noon.
