Lesson 4.03
⚜ Poser une question — Est-ce que, Inversion & Question Words
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
There are three ways to ask a question in French — and once you have them, you can ask about any schedule, any time, any day.
You already know the time words. Today you learn to ask: the easy est-ce que, the more formal inversion (as-tu…?), and how to lead with a question word (quand, pourquoi, combien, à quelle heure). Then you'll build a question yourself before you see the answer.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to form a yes/no question with est-ce que and with inversion (as-tu…?).
2
I will be able to ask an information question by leading with a question word (quand, à quelle heure…).
❔ PART 1 · RÈGLE N°1 — EST-CE QUE 10 min

The easiest way to ask

Rule 1. Put Est-ce que in front of a normal statement and you have a yes/no question — the word order doesn't change at all. Before a vowel it becomes Est-ce qu'.

Tu as cours aujourd'hui. (statement) → Est-ce que tu as cours aujourd'hui ? (question). Listen:

  • Est-ce que tu as cours aujourd'hui ?Do you have class today?
🔄 PART 2 · RÈGLE N°2 — L'INVERSION 10 min

Flip the verb and the subject

Rule 2. Swap the verb and the subject pronoun and join them with a hyphen: tu as → as-tu ? This is a little more formal, and it's what you hear in polite questions and in writing.

You met these subject pronouns back in Modules 1–2; here they just trade places with the verb. Listen:

  • As-tu cours le lundi ?Do you have class on Mondays?
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Take three statements you can already say (tu es français, tu as un chien, tu parles anglais) and turn each into a question two ways — once with est-ce que, once with inversion. Doing both trains your ear for which one sounds right where.
📋 PART 3 · LES MOTS INTERROGATIFS 12 min

Asking for information, not just yes/no

Lead with a question word, then follow it with est-ce que (or inversion). This is how you ask when, why, and at what time something happens.

Question wordMeaningExample
quandwhenQuand est-ce que le cours commence ?
à quelle heureat what timeÀ quelle heure finit l'école ?
pourquoiwhyPourquoi est-ce que tu te dépêches ?
combienhow much / how manyCombien de cours as-tu aujourd'hui ?

Hear a question word lead a full question:

  • Quand est-ce que le cours commence ?When does the class start?
✍️ PART 4 · À TON TOUR 8 min

Turn this statement — Le cours commence à huit heures — into a question three ways: with est-ce que, with inversion, and as an à quelle heure question. Commit to an answer first, then reveal to check. Trying before you see the answer is what makes it stick.

🧠 BUILD · three questions from one statement
Write the three questions, then reveal.
est-ce queEst-ce que le cours commence à huit heures ?
inversionLe cours commence-t-il à huit heures ?
à quelle heureÀ quelle heure est-ce que le cours commence ?
Note the little -t- in the inversion (commence-t-il) — French adds it to keep two vowels from crashing together. If you got the first two, you've got question formation cold.
