Grammar Quiz · L'heure & les questions
30 pts · 2 attempts
COMPLETED IN CANVAS · AUTO-GRADED 30 min
  • Listening items (~15 pts): hear a sentence, then answer — identify the clock time, the question word, or the meaning. Headphones recommended; you can replay each clip.
  • Grammar items (~15 pts): build a clock time (et quart, et demie, moins le quart, midi, minuit) and form a question (est-ce que, inversion, question words).
  • Two attempts — your higher score counts. Review any misses before your second try.
▶ To take the quiz: return to Module 4 in Canvas and open the Grammar Quiz — it is in the module’s item list, below the ten leçons.
🔎 PART 3 · RÉVISE TES ERREURS 5 min

After your first attempt, look at what you missed. Most Module 4 errors fall into three buckets: wrong clock ending (mixing up et quart / et demie / moins le quart), singular vs. plural heure(s), or question word choice (quand vs. à quelle heure vs. combien). Name which one caught you, fix that one thing, and take your second attempt.

🏁 CLÔTURE DU MODULE 4 — CE QUE TU SAIS FAIRE
  • I can name and understand 22 time-and-schedule words by ear.
  • I can tell any clock time with Il est ___ heure(s), et demie / et quart / moins le quart, midi, and minuit.
  • I can ask a question three ways — est-ce que, inversion, and with a question word (quand, pourquoi, combien, à quelle heure).
  • I can describe my own schedule in order, in writing and out loud.
  • I can read a French timetable on the 24-hour clock and understand a passage about someone's day.
