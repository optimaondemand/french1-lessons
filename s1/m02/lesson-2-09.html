Lesson 2.09
⚜ Carte de France & VR — Explore & Immerse
Lesson · about 40 minutes · ungraded practice
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A breath before the quiz — place France on the map, then step into a French classroom in VR.
Both of today's activities are ungraded practice. The map builds the geography you'll draw on all year (and feeds a per-semester extra-credit challenge); the VR session lets you use your school vocabulary in a real classroom before you're ever formally assessed on speaking.
🗺️ La Carte de France
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
EXPLORER + QUIZ MODES 13 min
  • Explorer mode: hover and click to learn the 13 metropolitan regions — Île-de-France, Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, Nouvelle-Aquitaine, and the rest.
  • Quiz mode: timed click-to-find. Your score, errors, and time are shown — restart as often as you like.
  • This is where you build the geography for the whole course. Mastering all 13 feeds the once-per-semester Carte extra-credit challenge in the capstone module.
▶ Open the Carte de France
🥽 Session VR · Une journée dans une salle de classe française
FORMATIVE · UNGRADED
ENGAGE · SOLO ACTIVITY 25 min
  • Enter the French-classroom Location in ENGAGE and move around: the desks, the board, the bookshelf, the teacher's desk.
  • At each object, say aloud what it is and what you do with it — le livre, le cahier, le stylo… j'étudie, j'écoute, je travaille.
  • Use the room as prompts: the board cues le professeur parle, the desk cues je travaille, the bookshelf cues les livres and les matières.
  • This is low-stakes rehearsal — no recording, no grade. Speak freely and make mistakes; that's the point.

Open ENGAGE from your headset or desktop app and join the Module 2 Location as directed in Canvas.

📝 PART 3 · NOTE RAPIDE 2 min

Tomorrow is the Grammar Quiz — the last graded piece of Module 2, and it includes listening items. Tonight, run the parler conjugation table once and listen to any three verbs on the Vocabulaire page. That's all the prep you need.

⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +8 min
Post-VR reflection: write 2–3 sentences about what felt natural and what tripped you up when you named objects in the classroom. Naming the hard part is how you fix it before the quiz.
