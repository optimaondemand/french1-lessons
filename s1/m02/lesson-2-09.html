⚡ Défi éclair
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
BUILD IT YOURSELF 5 min
Build each one in your head — or out loud — before you reveal it.
⚡ DÉFI 1
Say it in French: We speak French.
Nous parlons français.
nous takes -ons, and français needs no article after parler.
⚡ DÉFI 2
Say it in French: I study math.
J’étudie les maths.
je becomes j’ before the vowel, and French keeps les in front of maths.
⚡ DÉFI 3
Make it negative: Elle aime les devoirs.
Elle n’aime pas les devoirs.
ne and pas wrap the verb, and ne becomes n’ before a vowel.
