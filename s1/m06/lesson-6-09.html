⚡ Défi éclair
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
BUILD IT YOURSELF 5 min
Build each one in your head — or out loud — before you reveal it.
⚡ DÉFI 1
Say it in French: I am going to swim.
Je vais nager.
aller + the plain verb — that is the whole near future.
⚡ DÉFI 2
Say it in French: We are going to read.
Nous allons lire.
Only allons changes; lire stays as it is.
⚡ DÉFI 3
Say it in French: If the weather is nice, we go to the park.
S’il fait beau, on va au parc.
si becomes s’ before il, and on takes the same form as il.
