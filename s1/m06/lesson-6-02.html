Lesson 6.02
⚜ La météo & le verbe aller — Weather + “to go”
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you named the activities. Today you learn the one verb that gets you to them — and how to say what the sky is doing.
Weather decides so much of your free time, so you'll pick up the weather chunks first (il fait beau, il pleut) — whole phrases, learned as one piece. Then you meet aller (to go), the verb behind every plan: je vais à la piscine, on va au parc.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce the weather chunks and weekend words (words 12–22).
2
I will be able to conjugate aller in the present and match each form to its subject.
📚 PART 1 · LA MÉTÉO & LE WEEK-END 13 min

Eleven more words — listen, then repeat

The weather phrases are chunks: learn il fait beau as a single unit, like “it's-nice-out.” They all start with il fait… except il pleut and il neige. Don't worry about the verb faire inside them — the full faire paradigm comes in Semester 2; here weather is chunks only. Click the 🔊 and say each one back.

  • faire les magasinsto go shopping
  • allerto go
  • il fait beauthe weather is nice
  • il fait chaudit’s hot out
  • il fait froidit’s cold out
  • il pleutit’s raining
  • il neigeit’s snowing
  • quel temps fait-il ?what’s the weather like?
  • le week-endthe weekend
  • souventoften
  • le temps librefree time

🧠 PART 2 · LA GRAMMAIRE — LE VERBE ALLER 17 min

One irregular verb, endless plans

Aller means to go. It's irregular — you can't build it from a rule, you memorize it — but it's worth every minute, because you'll use it constantly. Here are all six forms:

SubjectForm of allerExample
jevaisje vais au parc
tuvastu vas à la piscine
il / elle / onvaon va au cinéma
nousallonsnous allons nager
vousallezvous allez danser
ils / ellesvontils vont au football

Hear the three you'll use most. Notice the form changes completely with the subject — that's what “irregular” means:

  • Je vais au cinéma.I am going to the movies.
  • Tu vas à la piscine ?Are you going to the pool?
  • Nous allons nager.We are going to swim.
✅ PART 3 · VÉRIFIE TA COMPRÉHENSION 10 min

Pick the form of aller that matches the subject. You get instant feedback — this is practice, not a grade.

✎ ITEM 1
Nous ______ au parc le samedi.
✎ ITEM 2
Tu ______ à la piscine cet après-midi ?
✎ ITEM 3
Elles ______ danser ce soir.
