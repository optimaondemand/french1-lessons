Lesson 6.03
⚜ Le futur proche — “I'm going to…” + Weather Plans
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
One small move turns aller into a time machine — and lets you make plans about the weekend.
Yesterday aller meant going somewhere. Today it does something bigger: put a second verb right after it and you're talking about the future — je vais jouer = “I'm going to play.” You'll also see how a weather chunk can open a plan (s'il fait beau, on va…). Then you'll build the table yourself.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to build the near future (aller + infinitive) to say what I'm going to do.
2
I will be able to conjugate aller through all six persons without help.
🕐 PART 1 · LE FUTUR PROCHE 10 min

aller + a second verb = the future

The rule. Conjugate aller for your subject, then add a second verb in its plain infinitive form (unchanged). je vais + jouer → je vais jouer = “I'm going to play.” It's the exact same shape as English “going to.”

Only aller changes; the second verb never does. Listen:

  • Il va jouer au football.He is going to play soccer.
  • Je vais regarder un film ce soir.I am going to watch a film tonight.
🌬️ PART 2 · LA MÉTÉO OUVRE UN PLAN 10 min

“If it's nice out, we go…”

The pattern. Start with a weather chunk, add a comma, then say the plan with aller: S'il fait beau, on va au parc. The little word s' is si (“if”) elided before il. Use quand (“when”) the same way.

Remember, the weather part is a fixed chunk — you're not conjugating faire yet (that's Semester 2). Listen for the two halves of each sentence:

  • S'il fait beau, on va au parc.If it's nice out, we go to the park.
  • Quand il pleut, je vais lire à la maison.When it rains, I read at home.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Write four “if/when” plans of your own, one for each weather chunk: S'il fait chaud, je vais… / Quand il neige, on va… Pairing a condition with a plan is exactly what the Speaking Task on Day 8 will ask you to do.
📋 PART 3 · LE TABLEAU COMPLET — ALLER 12 min

All six persons, one verb

This one table powers every plan in the module — both “going somewhere” and “going to do.” Read each row aloud; play the three you can hear.

✍️ PART 4 · À TON TOUR 8 min

Now build the near future of jouer (to play) through all six persons — on paper or in your head. Keep jouer unchanged; only aller moves. Commit to an answer first, then reveal to check.

🧠 CONJUGATE · le futur proche de jouer
Write all six near-future forms of jouer, then reveal.
je vais jouernous allons jouer
tu vas jouervous allez jouer
il/elle/on va jouerils/elles vont jouer
See how jouer never changed? Only aller moves. If you matched all six forms of aller, you've got the near future cold.
