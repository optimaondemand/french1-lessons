⚜ Phonétique & Speaking Task
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Sharpen the sound of aller, then speak — your own weekend plans, out loud, on the record.
First a quick clinic on the trickiest forms: the short singular vais / vas / va, the nasal vont, and the liaison in nous allons. Then you'll record your Speaking Task — the weekend you've been building all week.
🎤 PART 1 · PHONÉTIQUE 13 min

The short singular forms of aller

vais, vas, va are all short and crisp — one quick syllable. Don't stretch them. vais ends in an “eh” sound; vas and va end in an open “ah.” Keep the final consonants silent: you don't pronounce the -s.
  • je vais“veh” — silent -s
  • tu vas“vah” — silent -s
  • il va“vah” — open a

The nous liaison & the near future

nous allons links the words together: the silent -s of nous jumps forward as a “z” sound — “nou-z-allons.” In the near future (je vais regarder), vais and the second verb glide together with no pause.
  • nous allonsliaison: “nou-z-allons”
  • je vais regardernear future — smooth glide

Shadow it: play each clip, pause, and copy the exact rhythm three times. Aim to match the glide, not just the words.

🎤 Speaking Task · Ton week-end
15 pts · 1 submission
SUBMITTED IN CANVAS · MEDIA RECORDING 15 min
  • Record 6–8 sentences describing what you're going to do this weekend, in order.
  • Use at least five forms of the near future (je vais + verb), one weather chunk (s'il fait beau…), and souvent.
  • Speak from notes, not a script read word-for-word — natural delivery counts.
  • This is your Day 4 / Day 6 writing, spoken aloud. You've already built it.

How it's scored (15 pts):

✅ PART 3 · AVANT D'ENVOYER 12 min

Record once, listen back, and check yourself against this list. You may re-record before you submit — use that buffer.

□ I said 6–8 sentences.
□ Every plan has the right form of aller for its subject.
□ My second verb stayed an infinitive (je vais nager, not “je vais nage”).
□ I opened one plan with a weather chunk (s'il fait beau…).
□ I used souvent and kept vais/vas/va short.
□ I can hear my own words clearly on playback.
