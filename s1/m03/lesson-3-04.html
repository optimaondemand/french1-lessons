Lesson 3.04
⚜ Mise en pratique — Put Avoir to Work
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You know the verb. Today you use it — choosing, writing about your own family, and (for the first time) writing down what you hear.
Three moves, each harder than the last: pick the right form of avoir, write your own sentences, then take an audio dictation. Dictation is the bridge between hearing French and producing it — it's exactly what your listening-based Vocabulary Check rewards.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to choose the correct form of avoir in a full sentence and write my own sentences about my family.
2
I will be able to understand a spoken sentence with avoir well enough to write it down.
✅ PART 1 · CHOISIS LA BONNE FORME 12 min

Four sentences. Pick the form of avoir that fits the subject. Instant feedback — practice, not a grade.

✎ ITEM 1
J'______ un chien et un chat.
✎ ITEM 2
Ma sœur ______ huit ans.
✎ ITEM 3
Nous ______ une grande famille.
✎ ITEM 4
Vous ______ des cousins ?
✍️ PART 2 · À TOI D'ÉCRIRE 13 min

Write three sentences about your own family

In your notebook, write three true sentences using Module 3 words and avoir. Make one of them state an age (… a … ans) and try to mention a pet (un chien, un chat).

Model: J'ai une grande famille : un frère et une sœur. Mon frère a dix-huit ans. Nous avons aussi un chien !

Keep these — you'll reuse them for your Speaking Task on Day 8. Check each sentence against the avoir table before you move on.

🎧 PART 3 · LA DICTÉE 15 min

Hear it, write it, check it

Play each clip (as many times as you need). Write down exactly what you hear — accents and all — then reveal to check. Don't reveal early; the struggle is the learning.

1Play, write, then reveal.
J'ai un frère et une sœur.
2An age — listen for the number and ans.
Il a douze ans.
3Listen for the liaison in nous avons.
Nous avons une grande famille.
4Catch the difference: ils ont, not ils sont.
Ils ont un chat.
5Two idioms in one — hunger and thirst.
J'ai faim et j'ai soif.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +10 min
Spiral to Module 1: mix être and avoir. Write four sentences that contrast them — je suis américain, mais j'ai une famille française… Confusing être and avoir (especially for age) is the most common slip; practice it now.

✅ BEFORE YOU LEAVE
Click “Mark as Done” in Canvas. Keep your three sentences from Part 2 — they become the backbone of your Speaking Task.
💪 TU PRODUIS DU FRANÇAIS !

You didn't just recognize avoir today — you chose it, wrote it about your own family, and pulled it out of the air by ear. That's the whole skill in miniature. À demain !
