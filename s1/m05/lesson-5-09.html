⚡ Défi éclair
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
BUILD IT YOURSELF 5 min
Build each one in your head — or out loud — before you reveal it.
⚡ DÉFI 1
Say it in French: I get up at seven o’clock.
Je me lève à sept heures.
The pronoun me sits between je and the verb.
⚡ DÉFI 2
Say it in French: She gets dressed.
Elle s’habille.
se becomes s’ in front of the h of habiller.
⚡ DÉFI 3
Make it negative: Je me lève tôt.
Je ne me lève pas tôt.
ne goes before me, and pas after the verb — the pronoun stays inside.
