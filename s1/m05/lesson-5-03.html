Lesson 5.03
⚜ Les règles — Elision, Negation & the Full Table
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Two small rules make reflexive verbs sound French — and then you'll conjugate one all the way through, on your own.
Rule 1 was matching the pronoun. Today: what happens when the verb starts with a vowel (je m'habille), and how to say you don't do something (je ne me lève pas). Then you'll build the full table — and try one yourself before you see the answer.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to use elision before a vowel (me → m') and place the negation correctly (ne…pas around the pronoun + verb).
2
I will be able to conjugate a reflexive verb through all six persons without help.
🎵 PART 1 · RÈGLE N°2 — L'ÉLISION 10 min

When the verb starts with a vowel

Rule 2. Before a vowel sound, me, te, se drop their e and add an apostrophe: m', t', s'. French avoids two vowel sounds bumping together.

je me habille  ❌  →  je m'habille  ✅. Listen — you can hear the words glide together:

  • Je m’habille.I get dressed.
  • Il s’endort.He falls asleep.
🚫 PART 2 · RÈGLE N°3 — LA NÉGATION 10 min

Saying what you don't do

Rule 3. ne and pas wrap around both the pronoun and the verb: je ne me lève pas. The reflexive pronoun stays glued to the verb, inside the sandwich.

You met ne…pas back in Module 2. Here it just has one more word to jump over. Listen:

  • Je ne me lève pas tôt le samedi.I don’t get up early on Saturdays.
  • Elle ne s’habille pas vite.She doesn’t get dressed quickly.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Spiral back to Module 2: there, ne…pas wrapped only the verb (je ne mange pas). Write two sentences of each kind — one plain verb, one reflexive verb — and notice that the only difference is the little pronoun riding along inside.
📋 PART 3 · LE TABLEAU COMPLET — SE LEVER 12 min

All six persons, one verb

This is the model for every -er reflexive verb in the module. Read each row aloud; play the three you can hear.

PersonFormMeaning
jeje me lèveI get up
tutu te lèvesyou get up
il / elle / onil se lèvehe / she / one gets up
nousnous nous levonswe get up
vousvous vous levezyou (formal/plural) get up
ils / ellesils se lèventthey get up
  • Je me lève.je
  • Tu te lèves.tu
  • Il se lève.il / elle / on
✍️ PART 4 · À TON TOUR 8 min

Now conjugate se coucher (to go to bed) through all six persons — on paper or in your head. Commit to an answer first, then reveal to check. Trying before you see the answer is what makes it stick.

🧠 CONJUGATE · se coucher
Write all six forms of se coucher, then reveal.
je me couchenous nous couchons
tu te couchesvous vous couchez
il/elle/on se coucheils/elles se couchent
Same pattern as se lever — only the stem changed. If you matched all six pronouns, you've got Rule 1 cold.
