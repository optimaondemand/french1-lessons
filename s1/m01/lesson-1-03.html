Lesson 1.03
⚜ Les règles — Negation, tu vs vous & the Full Table
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Two rules make être do more — and one of them decides how respectful you sound to the person in front of you.
Rule 1 was matching the form. Today: how to say you are not something (je ne suis pas), and the choice at the heart of a good first impression — tu (friendly) versus vous (respectful). Then you’ll build the full table of être and try one yourself before you see the answer.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to make a sentence negative with ne…pas around être and choose tu or vous for the situation.
2
I will be able to conjugate être through all six persons without help.
🚫 PART 1 · RÈGLE N°2 — LA NÉGATION 10 min

Saying what you are not

Rule 2. To make a sentence negative, wrap the verb in ne…pas: je ne suis pas français (I’m not French). ne goes before the verb, pas right after it.

A few examples to read aloud:

  • Je ne suis pas professeur. — I’m not a teacher.
  • Elle n'est pas américaine. — She isn’t American. (ne becomes n' before the vowel in est.)
  • Nous ne sommes pas contents. — We’re not happy.

Notice: before a vowel, ne shortens to n' — the same gliding you hear all over French.

🤝 PART 2 · RÈGLE N°3 — TU OU VOUS ? 10 min

The choice that shapes your first impression

Rule 3. French has two words for you. Use tu (and tu es) with friends, family, and people your own age. Use vous (and vous êtes) with adults you don’t know, teachers, and anyone you want to show respect. When in doubt with someone new, choose vous.

Hear the difference — casual tu versus respectful vous:

  • Tu es français ?informal — a friend your age
  • Vous êtes madame Martin ?formal — an adult you’ve just met
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Make a two-column list: five people you’d address with tu (a classmate, a sibling…) and five you’d address with vous (a shopkeeper, a principal…). Getting a feel for the split now is what keeps you from sounding rude — or overly stiff — later.

All six persons of the most-used verb in French

✍️ PART 4 · À TON TOUR 8 min

Now write out all six forms of être from memory — on paper or in your head. Commit to an answer first, then reveal to check. Trying before you see the answer is what makes it stick.
