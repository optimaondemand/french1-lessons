⚜ Bonjour ! — Saying Hello
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
How do you make a good first impression in French — starting with the very first word out of your mouth?
Welcome to French 1. This week you learn to greet, thank, and take your leave — the words a French speaker hears in the first ten seconds of meeting you. Today you meet eleven of them, and you will hear each one spoken before you ever try to say it, because a greeting you can hear is a greeting you can use.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce eleven French greetings and courtesy words.
2
I will be able to match each spoken greeting to its meaning by ear.
📋 WHAT YOU NEED FOR THIS LESSON
▶
Headphones or speakers — today is a listening day.
▶
Your vocabulary notebook or flashcard app.
⏰ PART 1 · SE METTRE EN ROUTE 12 min

First, a word you already know

You have almost certainly heard bonjour before — in a film, a song, or a café. French greetings are among the most recognizable words in the world. This week we make them yours: not just recognized, but used, correctly, to the right person. It all begins with the impression you make in the first few seconds.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
You walk into a French bakery in the morning. What do you say first?
📚 PART 2 · LES SALUTATIONS 18 min

Listen, then repeat

Click the 🔊 on each word and say it back out loud. These eleven cover hello, goodbye, and the small courtesies (merci, de rien) that make you sound polite. Notice how many ways French has to say “see you” — each points to a different when.

  • bonjourhello / good day
  • saluthi / bye (informal)
  • bonsoirgood evening
  • au revoirgoodbye
  • à bientôtsee you soon
  • à demainsee you tomorrow
  • à plus tardsee you later
  • mercithank you
  • de rienyou’re welcome
  • s’il vous plaîtplease (formal)
  • ouiyes
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Go down the list a second time, but this time shadow each word: play it, pause, and repeat it out loud three times before moving on. Shadowing is the single fastest way to make new sounds feel natural.
📝 PART 3 · RÉVISE TON VOCABULAIRE 10 min

Drill the words with the Vocabulaire page

You don’t have to build your word bank from scratch. Open the Module 1 Vocabulaire study page (in this module) — every word, its meaning, and its audio in one place, as flip cards you can drill by ear. Because your Vocabulary Check is listening-based, practice by hearing the word and recalling its meaning.

Making your own paper or digital cards on top of that is a great memory booster — but it’s optional, not required.

💪 ON EST PARTI(E)S !

You now have the words that open every French conversation. Say one of them out loud right now, from memory, with the audio in your ear. That’s how a greeting becomes second nature — à demain !
