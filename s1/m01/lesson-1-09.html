⚡ Défi éclair
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
BUILD IT YOURSELF 5 min
Build each one in your head — or out loud — before you reveal it.
⚡ DÉFI 1
Say it in French: I am a student.
Je suis étudiant. / Je suis étudiante.
étudiante with an -e if you are female. Both are correct.
⚡ DÉFI 2
Greet your teacher in the evening, politely.
Bonsoir, madame. / Bonsoir, monsieur.
bonsoir after dark; madame or monsieur keeps it formal.
⚡ DÉFI 3
Make it negative: Elle est française.
Elle n’est pas française.
ne and pas wrap the verb, and ne becomes n’ before a vowel.
