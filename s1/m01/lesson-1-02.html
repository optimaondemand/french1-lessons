⚜ Se présenter — Introductions + the Verb être
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you learned to greet. Today you learn to introduce yourself — and meet the one verb that makes it possible.
You’ll pick up the words for names, titles, and “how are you,” then meet the most important verb in French: être, “to be.” Je suis, tu es, il est… — you need it to say who you are and who someone else is. Rule 1 is simply matching the right form of être to its subject.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to recognize and pronounce the introduction and courtesy words (words 12–22).
2
I will be able to match each form of the verb être to its subject.

Eleven more words — listen, then repeat

🧠 PART 2 · LA GRAMMAIRE — LE VERBE ÊTRE 17 min

The verb that says who you are

Être means to be. It’s how you say I am a student, she is French, we are friends. It is also irregular — you can’t predict its forms from a rule, so you memorize the six. That’s Rule 1: match the right form of être to its subject.

SubjectForm of êtreExample
jesuisje suis étudiant
tuestu es français
il / elle / onestelle est américaine
noussommesnous sommes amis
vousêtesvous êtes madame Martin
ils / ellessontils sont contents

Hear the three you’ll use most. Listen for how different each form sounds — there’s no shared pattern to lean on:

  • Je suis étudiant.I am a student.
  • Tu es français ?Are you French?
  • Elle est américaine.She is American.
