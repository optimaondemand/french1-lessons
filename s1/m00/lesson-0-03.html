Lesson 0.03
⚜ Les sons I — Silent Finals & Vowels
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
French words look longer than they sound. Today you learn why — and stop pronouncing letters that aren't there.
The single biggest key to sounding French: most final consonants are silent. Once your ear expects that, whole words click into place. Then we'll lock in the five vowel sounds that carry the language.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to apply the silent-final-consonant rule when I read a French word aloud.
2
I will be able to pronounce the five French vowels a, e, i, o, u.
🔇 PART 1 · LES LETTRES MUETTES 15 min
The rule. A final consonant is usually silent — you write it, you don't say it. (Handy exception: the letters in c-a-r-e-f-u-l — c, r, f, l — often are said at the end.) A final -e is also silent, but it “wakes up” the consonant right before it.

Listen — the last letter you see is not the last sound you hear:

  • petitsmall — final t silent (“puh-TEE”)
  • françaisFrench — final s silent
  • beaucoupa lot — final p silent (“boh-KOO”)
  • saluthi — final t silent (“sah-LU”)

Compare petit (“puh-TEE”) with petite (“puh-TEET”): the added -e makes the t sound. That silent -e is doing real work.

🎵 PART 2 · LES VOYELLES 14 min

The five vowels — pure and short

French vowels are crisp — they don't glide the way English vowels do. Play each and copy it exactly. The tricky one is U: round your lips as if for “oo,” but say “ee.”

A“ah”
E“euh”
I“ee”
O“oh”
Urounded “ee”
Two must-know combos: ou = “oo” (as in beaucoup), and é = the crisp “ay” from yesterday (as in café). You'll meet the nasal vowels tomorrow.
✅ PART 3 · VÉRIFIE TON OREILLE 11 min

Play the clip, then answer. Instant feedback.

Do you pronounce the final t in salut?
The final p in beaucoup is…
Which word is pronounced “puh-TEET” (with a final T sound)?
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +7 min
Play the four words in Part 1 again, one at a time, and write down the last sound you actually hear for each. Then say each word aloud, deliberately not pronouncing the silent letter. Training your mouth to drop it is the whole game.
