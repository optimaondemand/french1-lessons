⚜ Les accents — The Five Accent Marks
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Those little marks aren't decoration — they change how a letter sounds, and sometimes what a word means.
French has five accent marks. Today you'll learn what each one does and exactly how to type it — on Windows, Mac, and your phone — because you'll need them from your very first written word.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to name the five accent marks and say what each does to a letter.
2
I will be able to type any accented French letter on my own device.
📚 PART 1 · LES CINQ ACCENTS 13 min

Click the 🔊 to hear each accent's name in French.

AccentNameWhat it doesExample
é accent aigu Closes the e to a crisp “ay” sound. Only ever on e. café, école
è à ù accent grave On e, opens it to “eh.” On a/u, it doesn't change the sound — it separates meanings (a vs à, ou vs où). mère, là, où
ê â î ô û accent circonflexe Often marks a letter (usually an s) that history dropped: forêt = “forest.” hôtel, être, forêt
ç la cédille Makes c sound like s before a, o, u. Only ever on c. français, garçon
ë ï ü le tréma Tells you to pronounce two vowels separately, not as one sound. Noël, maïs
⌨️ PART 2 · COMMENT LES TAPER 16 min

Type accents on any device

You'll type French all year, so set yourself up now. Pick your device and try each one in a blank document.

DeviceHowExample: get é
Phone / tablet Press and hold the base letter on the on-screen keyboard; a row of accented options pops up. Slide to the one you want. Hold e → choose é
Mac Hold the base letter (same pop-up as phones), or use the option keys. ⌥+e, then e
Windows (easiest) Turn on the US-International keyboard (Settings → Time & Language → Language). Then type the accent key, then the letter. ' then e = é ·  ` then e = è
Windows (alt codes) Hold Alt and type the number on the numeric keypad. Alt+0233 = é ·  Alt+0231 = ç

On US-International, the accent keys are “dead keys”: press ', then the vowel. To type a normal apostrophe, press ' then space.
