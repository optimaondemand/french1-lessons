📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A quick sound tune-up — then you read French aloud and record it. Your first time speaking on the record.
Everything this module has been building toward: reading French out loud with a real accent. Warm up on the three sound rules, then record the Speaking Task.
🎤 PART 1 · PHONÉTIQUE 13 min

The three rules, in your mouth

1 · Silent finals. Don't say the last consonant. Play and copy:
  • salut“sah-LU” — no final t
  • petit“puh-TEE” — no final t
2 · Nasal vowels. Let the vowel hum; drop the n:
  • bonnasal “on”
  • grandnasal “an”
3 · Liaison. Link the silent consonant to the next vowel:
  • Comment allez-vous ?the t links: “koh-mahn-tah-lay-VOO”
🎤 Speaking Task · Lis à voix haute
15 pts · 1 submission
SUBMITTED IN CANVAS · MEDIA RECORDING 15 min

Record yourself reading the following aloud, clearly, in this order:

  • Spell your first and last name using French letter names.
  • Count from 1 to 10 in French.
  • Read these three words applying the sound rules: petit · français · beaucoup.
  • Read this phrase with its liaison: Comment allez-vous ?

How it's scored (15 pts):

CriterionPtsFull marks
Completion5All four parts, in order, clearly recorded
Letter & number accuracy5Correct French letter names and numbers 1–10
Pronunciation5Silent finals dropped, nasals hummed, the liaison linked
▶ Open the Speaking Task in Canvas & record
✅ PART 3 · AVANT D'ENVOYER 12 min

Record once, listen back, and check yourself. You may re-record before submitting.

□ I spelled my name with French letter names (not English).
□ I counted 1→10 without stumbling.
□ I dropped the silent final letters (petit, français, beaucoup).
□ I linked the liaison in Comment allez-vous ?
□ I can hear myself clearly on playback.
⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +8 min
Record-and-compare: play each phonics clip, then record yourself saying the same word, and listen back-to-back. Note one sound to polish. Imitating the model is the fastest route to a real accent.
