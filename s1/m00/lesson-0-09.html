Lesson 0.09
⚜ Carte de France & VR — Explore & Immerse
Lesson · about 40 minutes · ungraded practice
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A breath before the final quiz — meet the country you'll spend a year exploring, and step into the VR campus.
Both of today's activities are ungraded. The map gives you the geography this whole course lives in; the VR session gets you comfortable in the space where you'll practice speaking all year.
🗺️ La Carte de France — premier regard
PRACTICE · UNGRADED
EXPLORER MODE 13 min
  • Open the map and use Explorer mode: hover and click to see France's 13 metropolitan regions.
  • Notice France's six-sided shape — the French call their country l'Hexagone. Find Paris (in Île-de-France) near the top.
  • Don't worry about memorizing regions yet — just get oriented. The map grows with you: each module adds more to know, feeding a per-semester extra-credit challenge later.
▶ Open the Carte de France
🥽 Session VR · Bienvenue sur le campus
FORMATIVE · UNGRADED
ENGAGE · ORIENTATION 25 min
  • Enter ENGAGE and join the French 1 orientation Location as directed in Canvas.
  • Learn the basics: how to move, look around, and use your microphone. You'll use these all year for speaking practice.
  • Walk through the French classroom space. As you spot objects, try naming letters and numbers you see — a clock, a page number, a poster — out loud in French.
  • No recording, no grade — this is about getting comfortable in the space.

Open ENGAGE from your headset or the desktop app; if this is your first time, follow the setup guide linked in Canvas.

📝 PART 3 · NOTE RAPIDE 2 min

Tomorrow is the Pronunciation & Sound Quiz — the last graded piece of Module 0, and it includes listening items. Tonight, run the Module 0 Référence page once: letters, numbers, and days by ear.

⭐ POUR ALLER PLUS LOIN · optional · +8 min
Back on the map, find the region your VR classroom is “in” and one region on the coast. Say each region's first letter aloud in French. Small, but it links geography to sound.
