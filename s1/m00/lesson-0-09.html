⌨️ Écrire les accents — ton clavier
GARDE CETTE PAGE
COPIE-COLLE + TON APPAREIL garde-le sous la main
Les accents font partie du mot. café is not cafe, any more than cat is cot — the mark is part of the spelling, exactly like a letter. Build the habit now and every module after this one gets easier.
Select any character below and copy it. It works on every device, and needs nothing installed.
é à è ç ê ô î œ ù ë ï â ü û É À Ç È Ê Î Ô Ù
Faster, once you know your own keyboard:
  • Phone or tablet — press and hold the plain letter, then slide to the accented one. Hold e for é è ê ë, c for ç, a for à â, o for ô, u for ù û ü.
  • Mac — hold Option and a mark, release, then type the letter: Option+e then e gives é · Option+` gives è à ù · Option+i gives ê î ô · Option+u gives ë ï · Option+c gives ç.
  • Chromebook — add the US International keyboard in Settings › Languages and inputs. Then ' then e gives é · ` then e gives è · ^ then e gives ê · ' then c gives ç.
  • Windows — hold Alt and type the number on the number pad: é is Alt+0233 · è 0232 · ê 0234 · à 0224 · ç 0231 · ô 0244 · î 0238. No number pad? Copy from the strip above.
