Pronunciation & Sound Quiz
30 pts · 2 attempts
COMPLETED IN CANVAS · AUTO-GRADED 30 min
  • Listening items (~15 pts): hear a letter, number, day, or word and identify it, or say whether a final letter is pronounced. Headphones recommended; replay allowed.
  • Sound-rule items (~15 pts): silent finals, nasal vowels vs. liaison, and which accent does what.
  • Two attempts — your higher score counts. Review misses before your second try.
▶ To take the quiz: return to Module 0 in Canvas and open the Pronunciation & Sound Quiz — it is in the module’s item list, below the ten leçons.
🔎 PART 3 · RÉVISE TES ERREURS 5 min

After your first attempt, name what tripped you up — a letter name, a number, a sound rule — fix that one thing on the Référence page, and take your second attempt.

🏁 CLÔTURE DU MODULE 0 — CE QUE TU SAIS FAIRE
  • I can say the 26 French letter names and spell aloud.
  • I can name and type the five accent marks.
  • I can apply the core sound rules: silent finals, nasal vowels, liaison.
  • I can understand and say the numbers 1–30 and the days of the week.
  • I can read simple French aloud with a recognizable accent.
