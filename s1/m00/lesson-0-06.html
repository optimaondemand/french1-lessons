⚜ Les jours de la semaine & Révision
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
One last set of words — the days — then pull the whole week together before the graded work opens.
Learn the seven days, then run a fast review of everything from Week 1: letters by ear, numbers by ear, the sound rules. Tomorrow the assessments open, so today is your tune-up.
📅 PART 1 · LES JOURS 12 min
lundiMonday
mardiTuesday
mercrediWednesday
jeudiThursday
vendrediFriday
samediSaturday
dimancheSunday
Two French habits: the week starts on lundi (Monday), and days are not capitalized mid-sentence. Add le to mean “on ___s”: le samedi = “on Saturdays.” Notice five of the seven end in -di.
🔄 PART 2 · RÉVISION À L'OREILLE 16 min

A mix from all of Week 1. Play each, then answer — no peeking.

Which letter name is this?
Which number is this?
Which day is this?
The vowel sound in vingt is…
✍️ PART 3 · AUTO-TEST 12 min

Do all four out loud/on paper first, then reveal to check:

🧠 CHALLENGE · from memory
  1. Say the 7 days in order.
  2. Count 1→20.
  3. Spell MERCREDI aloud (French letter names).
  4. Which accent is on the “e” of café?
1. lundi, mardi, mercredi, jeudi, vendredi, samedi, dimanche · 2. un…vingt (check yourself with Lesson 0.05) · 3. “èm—é—èr—cé—èr—é—dé—i” (M-E-R-C-R-E-D-I) · 4. l'accent aigu (é).
