📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Don't review everything. Review your weak spots.
You have taken nine module checkpoints and the Midpoint Exam. Those scores tell you exactly where tomorrow's points are hiding. Today you look them up, build a plan from them, and spend your time where it counts.
📊 STEP 1 · CHECK YOUR SCORES 5 min
In Canvas, open Grades (in the course menu). For each Module Checkpoint, type your percentage below — your higher attempt is the one that counts. Leave a box empty if you didn't take that checkpoint.
Your scores stay on this computer only. Nobody else sees them.
🧭 STEP 2 · YOUR REVIEW PLAN 25 min
Type your scores in Step 1 and press Build my plan. Your plan will appear here, weakest module first.
✏️ STEP 3 · FIX YOUR MISTAKES 10 min
Open your two lowest checkpoints in Canvas. After your last attempt, Canvas shows the correct answers. Find three questions you got wrong and write each one down the right way — then say the French out loud.
