⚜ Grand, grande — Adjectives That Agree
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
In French, a describing word changes its ending to match the person it describes.
You've already said je suis français or je suis française. That small change is a rule, and it works for almost every describing word. Today you learn six describing words for people and the four endings that make them agree: masculine, feminine, and plural.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to add -e, -s or -es so a describing word agrees with the person it describes.
2
I will be able to describe members of my family with être and a describing word.
📚 PART 1 · SIX WORDS TO DESCRIBE PEOPLE 10 min

Listen, then repeat — out loud

Click the 🔊, then say each word back out loud, copying the voice as closely as you can. Each card shows the form for a man first, then the form for a woman.

  • grand / grandetall, big
  • petit / petiteshort, small
  • content / contentehappy
  • intelligent / intelligenteintelligent, smart
  • timideshy (same for both)
  • sympathiquenice, friendly (same for both)

You already know two more from Module 1: français / française and américain / américaine.

🧠 PART 2 · LA RÈGLE — FOUR ENDINGS 15 min

Start with the man's form, then add

The form for a man is the base. Add -e for a woman, -s for more than one, and -es for more than one woman.

One personMore than one
MasculineMon frère est grand.Mes frères sont grands.
FeminineMa sœur est grande.Mes sœurs sont grandes.

Three details make the rule complete:

  • Already ends in -e? Nothing changes for a woman: Mon père est timide. Ma mère est timide.
  • Already ends in -s? Nothing changes for the plural: Mon oncle est français. Mes oncles sont français.
  • A mixed group (men and women together) uses the masculine plural: Mes parents sont grands.
What you hear. Remember from Module 0: a final consonant is usually silent, but a final -e wakes it up. So petit ends “puh-TEE,” while petite ends “puh-TEET.” The -s of the plural stays silent: grand and grands sound the same.

Hear the pairs, and say each sentence back out loud:

  • Mon frère est petit.My brother is short.
  • Ma sœur est petite.My sister is short.
  • Mes cousins sont intelligents.My cousins are smart.
  • Mes cousines sont intelligentes.My (female) cousins are smart.

Where it goes: most describing words come after the noun (un élève intelligent). Grand and petit come before it — that's why you saw une grande famille and un grand chien in Lesson 3.02.

✅ PART 3 · VÉRIFIE TA COMPRÉHENSION 8 min

Pick the form that agrees. Go slowly: first ask man or woman?, then one or more? Instant feedback; this is practice, not a grade.

✎ ITEM 2

✎ ITEM 3

✎ ITEM 4

✎ ITEM 5

✎ ITEM 6

✍️ PART 4 · À TON TOUR 7 min

Describe three people in your family. For each one, give an age with avoir and a describing word with être — and make the describing word agree. Then say all three out loud. Commit first — then reveal a model.

Mon frère a quinze ans. Il est grand et timide.one man → grand
Mes grands-parents sont contents et intelligents.mixed group → -s
